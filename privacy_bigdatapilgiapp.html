Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Big Data Analysis Engineer Exam Study Notes (빅데이터분석기사 필기 합격노트) (the "App"). The Android application ID is com.seul.bigdatapilgiapp, and the documented App version is 3.0.0.

The App is a Korean-language study application designed to help users prepare for the Big Data Analysis Engineer examination. It provides offline learning materials, practice questions, flashcards, mock examinations, and progress tracking.

Important: Core learning content is available offline without registration. The App includes Google AdMob banner advertising on its home screen, which may involve data collection or processing by Google. The actual release build, SDK configuration, consent requirements, and Google Play Data safety disclosures should be checked before publication.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require an account or login. The documented implementation does not include a developer-operated analytics service or an account-based synchronization server. The App processes study-related information locally, such as reading progress, bookmarks, learning status, answers, mistakes, mock examination results, exam dates, and preferences.

Google AdMob may separately process device identifiers, advertising-related information, IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostic information, or other data according to its configuration and applicable Google policies. This information is not equivalent to the locally saved study records described below.

2. Practice Questions and Answers

The App contains 240 practice questions across four subjects and offers subject-based practice, random 20-question sessions, and review of incorrect answers. Question order and answer choices may be shuffled. Results from graded practice are used to calculate learning performance and update the local wrong-answer notebook. The "Show Answer" feature can reveal answers without grading or adding an incorrect-answer record.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App includes 30 learning units covering Big Data Analysis Planning, Big Data Exploration, Big Data Modeling, and Big Data Result Interpretation. Its documented learning materials include 796 frequently tested question-and-answer flashcards, 30 formula cards, 212 tables, and 38 graphs or diagrams. Learning materials are bundled with the App for offline use.

4. Memorization and Review

Users can mark flashcards and learning units as memorized, uncertain, or not known. These selections are stored locally to support filtering, review, and progress summaries.

5. Mock Examinations

Mock examinations contain 80 questions, with 20 questions from each of the four subjects. The App stores mock examination scores and subject-level results locally, with a documented history of up to 50 completed examinations. It can also save an unfinished examination session, including answers, question and choice order, and remaining time, to allow users to continue later.

6. Learning Records

The App maintains local learning records, including completed or read units, bookmarks, memorization status, incorrect-answer counts, consecutive correct answers, mock examination history, learning streaks, and selected settings. These records are used to display progress, identify weak areas, and support personalized review within the App.

7. Device Permissions

Core learning functions do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access. The advertising integration may require network access and advertising-related permissions or identifiers. Actual permissions depend on the release build and Android version.

8. Local Data Storage

The documented implementation uses Flutter's shared_preferences package to store study records and settings on the user's device. The App does not require an internet connection to read its bundled materials or access its local study features. Local data may be affected by device storage clearing, uninstallation, or operating-system backup and restore settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements on the home screen. AdMob is a Google service and may process information under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. For more information, see Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob guidance for app privacy disclosures.

The documented App does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting integration. This statement should be rechecked against the final release dependencies.

10. Advertising

The App displays a Google AdMob banner advertisement on the home screen. Advertisements may require internet connectivity. Depending on the user's region, consent choices, device settings, and AdMob configuration, Google may serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements and process advertising identifiers and related technical or interaction data.

Ad-related data processing is governed by Google's applicable policies. Users may have advertising privacy controls available through their device settings and any consent interface presented in the App.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The documented App does not use a separate developer-operated analytics service. However, the AdMob SDK may process diagnostic and advertising performance information. Any additional analytics or crash-reporting SDK introduced in a future release will be reviewed and reflected in this policy.

12. Data Sharing

Local study progress and memorization records are not designed to be uploaded to a developer-operated server. The App integrates Google AdMob, which may collect or receive advertising-related information as described in Sections 1, 9, and 10. The exact data categories and whether they are classified as collected or shared for Google Play purposes depend on the released SDK and configuration.

13. Data Security

Study records are stored locally and rely in part on device and operating-system security. Users should keep their devices updated and protect access to their devices. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Data processed by third-party advertising services is subject to those providers' applicable safeguards and policies.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Local study records are generally retained on the device until users reset their learning records, clear App storage, or uninstall the App, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior. The App provides a learning-record reset option in its study management settings. Resetting local study records does not necessarily delete information previously processed by Google AdMob; Google's own privacy controls and retention practices apply to such data.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is intended primarily for people preparing for a professional examination and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The App's target audience selections, advertising settings, and consent implementation must be configured consistently with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

16. User Choices

Users may use core learning features without registering an account. They can manage learning preferences and reset local study records through the App's study management settings. Device settings may offer additional controls for app data, advertising privacy, and uninstallation. Advertising consent choices, where required, depend on the final AdMob consent implementation.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when the App's features, integrated services, applicable laws, or store requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
