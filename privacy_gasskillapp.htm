The App is a Korean-language study application for the Gas Craftsman examination. Its core study content is designed to work offline and includes four study areas, 501 concept cards, CBT-style practice sets totaling 1,200 questions, memorization cards, 59 formulas, 63 comparison tables, 28 diagrams, eight engineering calculators, and study notes concerning KGS standards.

2. Practice Questions and Answers

The App offers CBT-style practice, 60-question mock exams, 20-question quick practice, 30-question calculation practice, sequencing exercises, and wrong-answer review. Whether complete answer histories are retained, and for how long, depends on the implemented local storage.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

4. Memorization and Review

5. Engineering Calculators

The App includes eight learning calculators covering cylinder quantity, pressure, liquid-column differential pressure, theoretical air requirement, explosion-related calculations, absolute pressure, relative gas density, and heating value. Inputs and results are used to perform calculations and link to related practice exercises. The project description does not establish that calculator inputs are transmitted to any remote service.
