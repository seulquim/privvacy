Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Electrician Craftsman Exam (전기기능사 필기) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.electricskillapp.

The App is an offline Flutter learning application for the Korean Electrician Craftsman examination. It includes 28 study units across three subjects, 331 learning cards, 4,560 questions organized into 76 practice/past-exam rounds, 128 unit questions, 105 formulas, and mock examinations.

Important: Learning progress, bookmarks, incorrect answers, scores, and exam-date preferences are stored locally on your device using shared_preferences. The App displays Google AdMob banner advertisements on its Home screen on supported mobile platforms. Ad providers may process technical and advertising information separately from locally stored study records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require users to register an account or provide their name, email address, telephone number, or contacts for its core learning features.

To provide its features, the App processes the following information locally:

  • Study units, completed learning cards, and bookmarks
  • Answers, scores, accuracy, and recent exam-round results
  • Incorrect-answer records and review progress
  • Selected examination date and D-day settings
  • Study preferences and locally saved progress

2. Quiz and Examination Practice

The App provides 4,560 questions organized into 76 rounds from 2002 through 2025, with 60 questions per round, as well as 128 unit practice questions. It also provides randomized practice and 60-question mock examinations covering the three subjects.

Question responses are processed locally to display scores, explanations, solution steps, learning points, common mistakes, and memorization tips. The App does not guarantee that its questions reproduce official examination papers verbatim.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App covers Electrical Theory, Electrical Machines, and Electrical Installations across 28 study units and 331 learning cards. Lessons may include diagrams, mathematical images, summaries, and completion markers.

Study content is bundled with the App for offline access and does not require a user account.

4. Flashcards and Memorization

Flashcards, bookmarks, and completed-card indicators support repeated review. The App may retain the user's learning status locally to resume study.

5. Formulas and Search

The App includes 105 subject-based formulas and provides search across study units, cards, and formulas. Search queries and formula viewing are handled within the App for its learning features.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App supports incorrect-answer review, subject progress, answer accuracy, recent round scores, and exam-date tracking. These records are stored on the device using local application storage.

7. Device Permissions

Network access may be used to load Google AdMob advertisements. The App's described learning features do not require camera, microphone, contact-list, or photo-library access.

Actual permissions and technical access depend on the final released build, operating system, and integrated SDKs.

8. Local Data Storage

The App uses shared_preferences to store progress, bookmarks, incorrect-answer records, scores, accuracy, and examination-date settings locally on the user's device.

Users can reset learning records and manage the examination date using App settings. Users may also clear App storage or uninstall the App through their device settings. Operating-system backups, if enabled, may retain certain local data.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob to display a banner advertisement at the bottom of the Home screen on Android and iOS. Advertising SDKs may process certain technical information in accordance with their own policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

The App includes Google AdMob banner advertising on its Home screen on supported Android and iOS devices. The supplied project description indicates that Google test advertising IDs are currently configured and must be replaced before production monetization.

When enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device and app information, advertising identifiers where available, approximate location inferred from network information, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic data under their applicable policies.

Advertising network communications are separate from the App's offline learning content and locally stored study progress.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project description does not establish that a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is enabled. Google AdMob and platform services may nevertheless process technical and diagnostic information as described in their policies.

If additional analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be revised as appropriate.

12. Data Sharing

The described core study features do not require uploading learning records to a Developer-operated account server. Advertising service providers may receive or process technical and advertising-related data through their SDKs.

We do not sell locally stored study records. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, to the extent it is available to the Developer.

13. Data Security

Study records are stored locally on the device. Users should maintain device security, operating-system updates, and appropriate backup settings.

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Advertising service communications are governed by the respective providers' security practices.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Local learning records generally remain until reset within the App, cleared through device settings, or removed by uninstalling the App, subject to any operating-system backup and restoration behavior.

Third-party advertising data is retained and deleted according to the relevant provider's policies and controls.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for people preparing for the Electrician Craftsman examination and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

The App does not require children to submit personal information through a Developer-operated account service. Advertising configuration must reflect the actual target audience and applicable child privacy requirements.

16. User Choices

Users may set or remove an exam date, reset learning records, clear the App's local storage, or uninstall the App.

Where supported, users can manage advertising identifiers and ad personalization preferences through their device or Google settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, its SDKs, applicable law, or app-store requirements. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
