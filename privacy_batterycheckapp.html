Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in 배터리 체크 ("the App").

배터리 체크 is an offline-first Android application for monitoring battery status and comparing the relative charging performance of cables under similar device and charger conditions.

Offline-first design: Battery readings, cable-test results, charging-session records, labels, notes, preferences, and related App data are stored locally on the user's device. The App does not require an account or SeulStudio cloud service for its core features.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App may process Android-provided battery information including battery level, charging status, power source, temperature, voltage, instantaneous and average current, and charge-counter information when supported. It may also store cable-test measurements, relative grades, cable labels, notes, charging-session history, alarm settings, themes, language, widgets, and monitoring preferences.

The App does not require a name, email address, phone number, postal address, or SeulStudio login for its core functions.

2. Local Storage

Battery history, cable-test results, labels, notes, settings, alarms, and related preferences are stored locally on the device. Core App data is not uploaded to a SeulStudio server during normal operation.

3. Battery Monitoring

The App reads battery information exposed by Android and device hardware. Availability and accuracy vary by manufacturer, Android version, battery state, temperature, charger, cable, screen activity, and charging controls. Unsupported current or charge-counter values may be shown as unavailable.

4. Cable Test

The 30-second cable test analyzes current and stability under the conditions present during the test and may assign an S/A/B/C/D relative grade. This is not an absolute certification of cable specifications. Results can also be affected by the charger, battery level, temperature, device workload, and device charging control.

5. Alerts and Notifications

The App may provide user-configured upper-limit, lower-limit, and overheating alerts, charging-connection notifications, and optional continuous-monitoring notifications. Depending on settings, alerts may use vibration, sound, or voice guidance. Android 13 or later may require notification permission.

An 80% alert does not physically stop charging. It recommends disconnecting the charger.

6. Foreground Monitoring

If explicitly enabled by the user, the App may use an Android foreground service and ongoing notification to continue battery monitoring while the App is not in the foreground. Continuous monitoring can be disabled by the user.

7. Charging Connection and Animation

The App may detect charging connection state and show a notification. It does not automatically force a full-screen interface from the lock screen. The charging animation screen opens through the relevant notification or App flow.

8. Home Screen Widgets

The App provides Android home-screen widgets in multiple sizes. Widgets may display locally available battery information. Widget data is not uploaded to a SeulStudio server.

9. Advertising

Advertising is controlled by an App configuration flag and is currently disabled by default. When enabled, the App may display Google AdMob banner advertisements. The App is designed to handle the applicable UMP consent state before initializing advertising and requesting ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising-related information under their own policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/

10. Network Use

Core battery monitoring, cable testing, records, charts, alarms, themes, and widgets are designed around locally available device data. Network communication may occur when advertising is enabled and Google AdMob/UMP services are used.

11. Permissions and System Access

  • Notification permission for alerts and monitoring notifications
  • Foreground-service capabilities for user-enabled continuous monitoring
  • Battery and charging-state information exposed by Android
  • Vibration, sound, or voice output for enabled alerts
  • Home-screen widget functionality

12. Data Sharing

SeulStudio does not sell the user's locally stored battery history, cable-test results, labels, or notes. When advertising is enabled, limited technical or advertising data may be independently processed by advertising services.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored App data may remain until the user deletes supported records, clears App storage, resets the device, or uninstalls the App. Advertising information, when applicable, is retained according to Google's policies.

14. Data Security

The App minimizes unnecessary transmission by keeping core battery and cable-test data on the user's device. No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

15. Accuracy and Functional Limitations

Important: Battery and current information depends on values exposed by Android and the device manufacturer. Some devices may not provide current or charge-counter data.

Current values represent net current entering or leaving the battery and are not direct laboratory measurements of cable specifications. Cable grades are relative comparisons intended for similar test conditions. The App cannot physically stop charging.

16. Children's Privacy

The App does not require a SeulStudio account or centralized user profile and is not designed to collect children's personal information through such an account.

17. User Choices

  • Enable or disable battery alerts
  • Enable or disable continuous monitoring
  • Manage notification permission in Android settings
  • Choose vibration, sound, or voice guidance
  • Delete supported local records
  • Clear App data or uninstall the App
  • Manage advertising consent and preferences where applicable

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in App functionality, applicable law, Google Play policies, or third-party services. The "Last updated" date will be revised when this policy changes.
