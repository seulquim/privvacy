Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or the "Developer") handles information in Automotive Maintenance Craftsman Master (자동차정비기능사 필기) (the "App"). The project name is autostudyapp, the Android application ID is com.seul.autostudyapp, and the described release version is 21.50.0.

The App is a Korean-language study application for the Automotive Maintenance Craftsman examination. It provides offline study materials, 71 sets of historical exam questions, 716 concept cards, 1,256 concept quizzes, 12 automotive calculators, a memorization tool, a wrong-answer notebook, and a practical-exam guide.

Before publishing, verify the release build and update this policy and the Google Play Data safety form to match actual behavior. The project is currently marked RELEASE_BLOCKED.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App processes study-related information needed to provide its features, such as exam attempts, scores, progress, incorrect answers, memorization/review status, and preferences. The supplied project description identifies local storage through SharedPreferences. It does not identify an account-registration requirement or a developer-operated server; the final build must be checked before making definitive claims about network collection.

2. Practice Questions and Answers

The App provides 71 historical exam sets and concept quizzes. It can process selected answers, grading results, and recent attempt scores to show performance and support review. The source inventory includes 4,260 historical question records, of which 4,238 are active and 22 are excluded from standard practice under the project's content policy. The precise persistence and reset behavior of individual attempts should be verified in the release build.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

Offline learning content covers automotive engines, chassis, electrical/electronic systems, and safety. The App includes 716 concept cards, 1,256 concept quizzes, 85 formulas, 78 reference tables, 27 diagrams, and practical-exam preparation guidance. These bundled educational materials do not inherently require users to submit personal information.

4. Memorization and Review

The memorization feature uses learning-status choices such as known, uncertain, and unknown to schedule review intervals. These selections and associated review schedules may be stored locally as part of the App's study state.

5. Automotive Calculators

The App provides 12 automotive engineering calculators, including engine displacement, compression ratio, braking distance, hydraulic force, steering ratio, and electrical calculations. Inputs are used to calculate and display results. The supplied description does not establish that calculator inputs are transmitted to an external service.

6. Learning Records

The App includes exam-score history, progress tracking, and a wrong-answer notebook. Users can remove mastered items from the wrong-answer list. Other history deletion or reset controls must be verified in the final build.
