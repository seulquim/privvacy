Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or the "Developer") handles information in Construction Safety Engineer Exam (건설안전기사 필기노트) (the "App"), package ID com.seul.constsafepirgiapp, version 10.0.0.

Important: The App provides offline study content with 820 learning cards across five subjects and 1,064 practice questions. Study progress and preferences are designed to remain on the device. Google AdMob may process technical and advertising information separately from study records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require account registration or submission of a name, email address, phone number, or contacts for its core learning features. Learning progress, card mastery (known/uncertain/unknown), answers, scores, incorrect-answer history, exam date, and preferences may be stored locally on the device.

2. Quiz and Examination Practice

The App provides 1,064 practice questions, randomized 20-question practice (four questions per subject), and practice sets A (120 questions) and B (100 questions). Answers are evaluated on the device, with explanations, related learning concepts, and incorrect-answer review. These are learning exercises and are not represented as official exam papers.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App includes 820 learning cards across five subjects, as well as formulas, explanatory tables, occupational safety signs, and safety manager appointment criteria. The learning content is bundled for offline use.

4. Flashcards and Memorization

Users can mark learning cards as Known, Uncertain, or Unknown, reveal answers and highlighted memorization points, and practice with concealed highlights. These learning states may be retained locally.

5. Formulas, Tables, and Search

The App supports subject filtering, local content search, formula viewing, and enlarged tables with value explanations. Searches are used within the App for learning purposes.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App tracks subject progress, memorization status, incorrect answers, learning streaks, and exam D-day. These records are intended to remain in local application storage.

7. Device Permissions

Internet access may be used to load Google AdMob advertisements. The described core learning features do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access. Actual permissions depend on the final released build and SDKs.

8. Local Data Storage

The App is designed to keep study progress, answers, review states, and exam-date preferences on the device. The exact storage mechanism should be confirmed against the final implementation. Users can reset records within the App or clear App data through device settings. Device backups may preserve some records.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob for a banner advertisement at the bottom of the Home screen. Advertising providers may process technical and advertising information under their own policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

The App displays a 320 × 50 Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the Home screen. The supplied project configuration uses Google test IDs, which must be replaced with production IDs before release. Depending on SDK settings and consent, advertising providers may process IP addresses, device/app details, advertising identifiers, approximate network-derived location, interactions, and diagnostics.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project information does not confirm a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google AdMob and operating-system services may process diagnostic or technical information under their respective policies. This policy should be updated if further SDKs are added.

12. Data Sharing

The described core learning functions do not require uploading study records to a developer-operated server. Advertising service providers may receive technical and advertising-related data through their SDKs. The developer does not sell locally stored study records.

13. Data Security

Study records are intended to be kept on the device. Users should keep their devices and operating systems secure. No storage or transmission system is completely secure. Advertising traffic is governed by the respective providers’ security practices.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Local study records generally remain until reset in the App, cleared through device settings, or removed on uninstall, subject to device backup and restoration. Third-party advertising information is retained and deleted according to the relevant provider’s policies.

15. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for people preparing for the Korean Construction Safety Engineer examination and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Advertising settings must match the actual target audience and applicable child privacy requirements.

16. User Choices

Users may configure the examination date, reset study records, clear local App data, or uninstall the App. Where supported, advertising personalization and identifiers can be managed through device or Google settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the App, SDKs, applicable law, or app-store requirements. The revision date will be updated accordingly.
