Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 층간기록 ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in 층간기록 ("the App").

층간기록 is a privacy-focused photo editing application designed to detect faces and potentially sensitive information in images and allow users to obscure those areas using mosaic, blur, solid-color masking, noise, stickers, and other editing tools.

Important: 층간기록 does not upload photos selected by users to servers operated by 층간기록. Face detection, text recognition, sensitive-data detection, and image editing are designed to be performed on the user's device whenever supported by the underlying platform and SDKs.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Processed by the App

층간기록 does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect or store personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, contacts, or user photos on servers operated by 층간기록.

When a user selects a photo, the App may process that photo locally on the device to provide features including:

  • Face detection
  • Optical character recognition (OCR)
  • Detection of possible Korean resident registration number patterns
  • Detection of possible payment card numbers, including Luhn validation
  • Detection of phone number patterns
  • Detection of email addresses
  • Detection of vehicle registration number patterns
  • Detection of possible bank account number patterns
  • Manual selection of areas to obscure
  • Image editing, saving, and export

Automated detection may produce false positives or fail to detect some information. Users should review the final edited image before saving, exporting, or sharing it.

2. Photo and Image Processing

Photos selected by the user are processed in device memory or local storage accessible to the App for the purpose of providing privacy editing functionality.

The App's core image editing functionality does not upload the user's original or edited photos to servers operated by 층간기록.

When the user chooses to save an edited image, the result may be stored in the device's photo gallery or in App-specific local storage.

If saving to the system gallery is not available due to device or operating system restrictions, the App may use an App-specific storage location as a fallback.

3. Face Detection

층간기록 may use Google ML Kit or similar on-device technologies to detect the location of faces in user-selected images.

Face detection is used only to identify areas that the user may wish to obscure using mosaic, blur, masking, or other privacy tools.

The App does not operate a facial recognition database and does not attempt to identify a person by name or associate a detected face with a specific individual.

층간기록 does not store face images or face detection results on its own servers.

4. OCR and Sensitive Information Detection

층간기록 may use Google ML Kit text recognition or similar technology to analyze text contained in an image.

Recognized text may be evaluated locally to identify potential sensitive information, including:

  • Korean resident registration number patterns
  • Payment card number patterns
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Vehicle registration numbers
  • Possible bank account numbers

Some detections are based on pattern-matching rules or validation algorithms and may identify text that is not actually sensitive information.

OCR results and recognized sensitive text are used for the App's editing functionality and are not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by 층간기록

5. Editing Features

Users may apply privacy protection to detected or manually selected areas using features including:

  • Mosaic
  • Blur
  • Solid-color masking
  • Noise masking
  • Sticker masking
  • Manual rectangular selections
  • Manual elliptical selections

Users may include or exclude automatically detected regions and may use undo and redo functionality during editing.

6. Image Saving and Metadata

Edited images may be saved in formats such as JPEG or PNG.

The App is designed to remove certain metadata, including some EXIF metadata, by re-encoding the image during export.

However, removal of every possible metadata field cannot be guaranteed across all devices, operating systems, image formats, or future App versions.

Users should independently verify highly sensitive images before sharing them outside the device.

7. Device Permissions

Depending on the device and operating system, the App may request access to or use system functionality required for:

  • Selecting photos or media
  • Saving edited photos
  • Using the system Photo Picker
  • Accessing permitted device storage locations

On supported Android and iOS versions, the App may prefer system-provided photo selection mechanisms instead of requesting broad access to the user's entire photo library.

8. Local Data Storage

The App may store settings, editing state, preferences, and other App-related data locally on the user's device.

This local data is used only to provide or improve the App's functionality.

Users may delete local App data by:

  • Using deletion features available in the App
  • Clearing the App's storage through the operating system
  • Uninstalling the App

9. Third-Party Services

The App may use services or SDKs provided by Google or other third parties to provide certain functionality.

Services currently used or that may be used in future versions include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google ML Kit
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Crashlytics, if enabled
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, if enabled

These third-party services may process certain technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google ML Kit Terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Firebase Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

10. Advertising

층간기록 may display advertisements through Google AdMob or similar advertising services.

If advertising services are enabled, advertising providers may process information such as:

  • Device information
  • Operating system and App version
  • Advertising ID, where available and permitted
  • Approximate location
  • Ad interaction information
  • App usage and diagnostic information

Network communications performed by advertising SDKs are separate from the App's photo editing and privacy detection functionality.

The App is not designed to provide user-selected photos, OCR-recognized text, detected faces, or sensitive-information coordinates to advertising providers for advertising purposes.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App may use analytics or crash reporting services in future versions to improve stability, performance, and usability.

If such services are used, the App is designed so that the following sensitive information should not be intentionally included in analytics events or crash reports:

  • User-selected original photos
  • Local photo file paths
  • Actual OCR-recognized text
  • Exact face or sensitive-data coordinates
  • Resident registration numbers
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Bank account numbers

12. Data Sharing

층간기록 does not sell user photos or sensitive information detected within photos.

Third-party service providers may process limited technical information when necessary to operate, maintain, analyze, improve, or monetize the App. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's privacy policy and applicable law.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal process. However, because 층간기록 does not operate a centralized server storing user photos or OCR results for the App's core functionality, we cannot provide information that we do not possess.

13. Data Security

층간기록 is designed to perform as much photo processing as reasonably possible on the user's device in order to reduce unnecessary transmission of sensitive content.

However, no method of electronic storage, processing, or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Users should take appropriate precautions when storing or sharing highly sensitive images.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

층간기록 does not retain user photos, OCR-recognized text, or face detection data on its own servers as part of the App's core image editing functionality.

Data stored locally on the user's device may remain until the user deletes it, clears App storage, deletes saved images, or uninstalls the App.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob or Firebase is retained and deleted according to the privacy policies and retention practices of those providers.

15. Children's Privacy

층간기록 is not primarily directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 through servers operated by 층간기록.

If the App is later made available to children under 13 or becomes part of a child-directed target audience, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement additional measures required by applicable child privacy laws, Google Play policies, and other relevant platform requirements.

16. User Choices

Users may manage App-related privacy settings through their device, including:

  • Changing photo access permissions
  • Changing storage-related permissions
  • Deleting locally stored App data
  • Deleting saved edited images
  • Resetting or deleting the Advertising ID
  • Limiting personalized advertising where supported
  • Uninstalling the App
