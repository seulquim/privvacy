Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in 층간기록 ("the App").

층간기록 is a local-first mobile application designed to help users record, organize, review, and export information related to inter-floor noise events. Depending on the user's choices, a record may include estimated noise measurements, original audio, optional video, date and time, location or address information, event markers, notes, response history, integrity hashes, and generated reports.

Local-first design: The App is designed so that core records and evidence files are stored on the user's device. The App does not require an account and does not operate a central server for storing users' noise recordings, videos, reports, or event records. Files leave the device only when the user actively exports, backs up, or shares them, except for limited technical data that may be processed by third-party advertising services as described below.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App may process the following information when the user enables or uses the corresponding features:

  • Microphone audio captured during a noise recording session
  • Estimated sound level information derived from microphone input
  • Minimum, maximum, average, and equivalent noise values generated by the App
  • Event markers such as footsteps, children running, door closing, furniture movement, instruments, or other user-selected categories
  • Optional video captured or selected by the user
  • Date and time associated with a recording or event
  • Optional location, address, or place information entered or permitted by the user
  • User-entered notes, tags, and response or contact history
  • Locally generated file integrity information such as SHA-256 hashes
  • Locally generated PDF, CSV, ZIP, backup, and report files
  • App settings and preferences

The App does not require users to provide a name, account, email address, phone number, or other profile information in order to use its core recording features.

2. Microphone and Audio Recording

The App may request microphone access so that users can record environmental sound associated with a noise event and estimate relative sound levels from the microphone signal.

Audio recordings are designed to be stored locally on the user's device. The App's core recording functionality does not upload original audio to servers operated by SeulStudio.

Estimated dB or equivalent-noise values generated by the App are based on the capabilities and calibration characteristics of the device microphone and are not guaranteed to be equivalent to measurements from a certified sound-level meter.

3. Optional Video Recording

If the user enables video evidence features, the App may request camera and related media permissions to capture or store video associated with a noise event.

Where implemented, the App may store audio and video as separate evidence files and may calculate hashes for those files to help the user verify whether a file has been altered after capture.

Video is not required for all App functions. Users should only record video when permitted by applicable law and when they have a lawful basis to capture the scene.

4. Location and Address Information

The App may allow users to attach location, address, or place information to a noise event. Depending on the device and App configuration, this may be entered manually or obtained through device location services with the user's permission.

Location information used for event records is designed to be stored locally with the related record unless the user chooses to export, back up, or share that record.

5. Event Markers, Notes, and Response History

Users may add event markers, tags, notes, and response history to help organize an incident. Examples may include contact with a building management office, another resident, police, a noise dispute center, mediation body, attorney, or other party.

These entries are created by the user and are intended for personal organization and documentation. The App does not independently verify the accuracy of user-entered notes, labels, allegations, or conclusions.

6. Local Storage

Core App data is designed to be stored locally on the user's device, including:

  • Noise event records
  • Audio and optional video evidence files
  • Event markers and notes
  • Response history
  • Settings and preferences
  • Generated reports and export files
  • File-integrity hashes

Local data may remain on the device until the user deletes it, clears App storage, deletes exported files, restores the device, or uninstalls the App.

7. Export, Backup, and Sharing

The App may allow users to export records as PDF, CSV, ZIP, backup archives, or similar files. Exported packages may contain information selected by the user, including recordings, videos, location or address data, notes, timestamps, markers, response history, and integrity information.

The App does not automatically send these files to third parties. Files leave the App's local storage only when the user initiates an export, backup, share, copy, or similar action, or when the operating system stores them in a user-selected location.

Once a user shares or saves a file outside the App, that file may be handled by the operating system, cloud-storage provider, messaging service, email service, receiving person, or other third party selected by the user. Their privacy practices are not controlled by SeulStudio.

8. File Integrity Information

The App may calculate cryptographic hashes such as SHA-256 for locally stored audio, video, or exported evidence files. A hash can help detect whether a file has changed after the hash was created.

A file hash does not by itself establish legal admissibility, authenticity, ownership, or the truth of the recorded event.

9. Device Permissions

Depending on the features used and the operating system, the App may request access to system functions such as:

  • Microphone access for audio recording and noise estimation
  • Camera access for optional video evidence
  • Photo or media access when importing or saving supported files
  • File or storage access for reports, backups, and exports
  • Location access when the user chooses to attach location to a record
  • Notifications, if reminder or status features are enabled

Users can manage App permissions through the device operating system. Some features may not work if the required permission is denied.

10. Advertising

The App may display banner advertisements through Google AdMob.

If advertising is enabled, Google or its advertising partners may process limited technical information such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • App version
  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted
  • Approximate location derived by the advertising service, where permitted
  • Ad interaction information
  • App usage and diagnostic information related to advertising delivery

Advertising SDK communications are separate from the App's local evidence-recording functionality. The App is not designed to provide users' original audio recordings, video evidence, event notes, exact event locations, or generated evidence packages to advertising providers for advertising purposes.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

11. Data Sharing

SeulStudio does not sell users' noise recordings, videos, event records, notes, or location information.

User-created records may be disclosed to third parties only when the user chooses to export or share them, or where disclosure is otherwise required by applicable law or valid legal process and the Developer actually possesses the relevant information.

Because the App is designed without a centralized SeulStudio server for users' evidence records, the Developer generally cannot provide copies of local records that remain only on the user's device.

12. Data Security

The App is designed to reduce unnecessary transmission of sensitive evidence by keeping core records on the user's device.

However, no electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their devices, device backups, exported evidence files, and any copies they choose to share.

If the App provides local backup, PIN, biometric, encryption, or similar protections in a particular version, users should still maintain appropriate device-level security and secure handling of exported files.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by the App may remain until the user deletes the record, clears App storage, removes exported files, or uninstalls the App.

Exported files copied to other folders, cloud drives, messaging apps, email services, or recipient devices must be deleted separately from those locations.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to the policies of those providers.

14. Legal and Safety Considerations

Important: Laws regarding audio recording, video recording, location information, personal data, surveillance, evidence collection, and sharing may differ by jurisdiction and situation. Users are responsible for determining whether their recording and sharing activities are lawful.

The App does not determine whether a person committed a crime, violated a lease, caused a specific noise event, or bears legal responsibility for an incident.

Noise measurements, pattern summaries, labels, markers, and generated reports are intended to help users document and organize events. They are not a substitute for a certified sound-level measurement, professional investigation, legal advice, or an official determination by a court, public authority, building management body, or dispute-resolution organization.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is not primarily directed to children under the age of 13.

SeulStudio does not knowingly operate a server that collects children's audio, videos, event records, or location information as part of the App's core functionality.

16. User Choices

Users may manage their privacy by:

  • Granting or denying microphone, camera, media, location, and notification permissions
  • Choosing whether to enable optional video or location features
  • Deleting individual event records and evidence files
  • Deleting local backups and exported files
  • Clearing the App's local storage
  • Managing advertising preferences where supported by the operating system or advertising provider
  • Uninstalling the App

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page will be revised.
