Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Information Processing Engineer (정보처리 기사) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.infoengpirgiapp.

The App is a Flutter-based offline study aid for the Korean Information Processing Engineer examination, featuring 257 concepts, 3,764 independently created CBT questions, mock examinations, memorization tools, 39 formula/table items, and incorrect-answer review.

Important: Learning records and preferences are stored locally on the device using shared_preferences. Core study features work offline. A Google AdMob banner on the Home screen may process advertising-related information independently of local study records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require an account or login. Its learning features do not require users to submit their name, email address, telephone number, or contacts to the Developer. The following are processed locally: study progress and memorization status (memorized / unsure / not memorized); quiz responses, scores, subject-level results and incorrect-answer history; selected exam date and D-day; font-size settings and learning preferences.

2. CBT Practice and Mock Examinations

The App includes 3,764 independently created CBT-style questions, a 100-question mock examination with 20 questions per subject, 20-question subject practice, and a daily set of 10 questions. OMR-style navigation, automatic scoring, subject-level minimum-score checks and incorrect-answer review are performed on the device. Study responses and results are stored locally for learning continuity.

3. Learning Materials and Study Content

The App provides 257 independently study concepts across five subjects: Software Design, Software Development, Database Construction, Programming Language Utilization, and Information System Construction Management. It includes 823 highlighted keyword occurrences and 39 formulas or comparison tables. Study content is bundled with the App and can be used offline.

4. Memorization and Keyword-Hiding Features

Users may hide highlighted keywords, reveal answers, and mark concepts as memorized, unsure, or not memorized. These choices are stored on the device for repeated learning and review.

5. Formulas and Tables

The App includes 39 formula and comparison-table items that users can enlarge and pan for easier study. These materials are accessed locally and do not require uploading user inputs to a Developer-operated server.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App tracks study progress, exam results, incorrect answers, memorization status, and a user-selected examination date. Users can reset memorization status and incorrect-answer records separately through the App settings. The records are stored locally on the device.

7. Device Permissions

Core learning features operate offline. Internet access may be used to load a Google AdMob banner on the Home screen when advertising is enabled. The described study features do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access. Actual permissions, including those added by advertising SDKs, should be verified in the release build.

8. Local Data Storage

The App uses shared_preferences to store study progress, memorization status, incorrect-answer history, exam date, font-size selection, and other settings on the device. The Developer does not operate an account server for uploading these learning records. Users can reset supported records in the App, clear App storage in operating-system settings, or uninstall the App. Device backups may retain local data according to operating-system settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob to display a banner advertisement on the Home screen of supported mobile devices. Google and its partners may process advertising and technical information under their own policies. See Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

When advertising is enabled, a Google AdMob banner is displayed at the bottom of the Home screen. Ads use network access separately from the offline learning features. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device identifiers (including advertising IDs where available), approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, app information, and diagnostics under their policies and applicable consent settings. The project currently uses test ad identifiers that must be replaced before production monetization.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project description does not identify a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google AdMob and operating-system services may process diagnostics and technical information under their own policies. This section will be revised if additional analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced.

12. Data Sharing

The Developer does not operate a user-account system or a backend service for uploading locally stored learning records. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may receive or process technical and advertising-related data through their SDKs under their own privacy policies. Information may be disclosed where legally required, to the extent available to the Developer.

13. Data Security

Learning records and preferences are stored on the device. Users should maintain appropriate device security and backup settings. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Advertising communications are subject to the security practices of the relevant third-party providers.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored study records may remain until the user resets them through available App functions, clears App data, or uninstalls the App, subject to device backup behavior. Data processed by Google AdMob or platform providers is subject to those providers’ retention and deletion policies.

15. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for people preparing for the Korean Information Processing Engineer examination and is not specifically designed for children under 13. It does not request children’s personal information through an account-registration system. The Developer must configure age-related advertising settings according to the actual target audience and applicable requirements.

16. User Choices

Users can manage memorization status, examination date, font size, and learning records in the App. They can reset supported learning data, clear App storage, or uninstall the App. Where available, users may manage advertising identifiers and ad personalization through device or Google settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, advertising SDKs, applicable laws, or platform requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
