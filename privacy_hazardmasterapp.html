Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Hazardous Materials Industrial Engineer Exam (위험물산업기사 필기) (the "App"), Android package ID com.seul.hazardmaster and iOS bundle ID com.seul.hazardmasterapp, version 2.2.0 (build 22).

The App is a Korean-language offline Flutter learning application for the Hazardous Materials Industrial Engineer examination. It covers three subjects through 38 core concepts, an interactive periodic table of 118 elements, updated chemical terminology, hazardous-materials transportation standards, and 136 educational practice questions.

Important: Core learning content is available offline. The App keeps study progress, bookmarks, incorrect-answer history, and study preferences on the device. A banner advertisement appears on the Home screen. The advertising provider and the exact data processed by its SDK should be verified against the released build. Advertising services may process technical or advertising-related data independently of local study records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App's core learning features do not require account registration or submission of a name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

  • Completed concepts, subject-level progress, and last-read study location
  • Bookmarks and study preferences
  • Answers to practice questions, results, and incorrect-answer records
  • Information needed to resume reading and review incorrect answers

2. Quiz and Examination Practice

The App includes 136 educational practice questions: 76 questions associated with the 38 core concepts and 60 additional questions covering topics such as the periodic table, chemical terminology, and transportation standards. Users can practice by study range, view explanations, revisit related concepts, and review incorrect answers. Responses and review records are processed to provide these features. The questions are for learning and are not represented as official examination papers.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App includes 55 content entries: four introductory or guide entries, 38 core concepts across three subjects (12, 9, and 17 respectively), a periodic table covering 118 elements, a chemical terminology update page, and 11 pages addressing hazardous-materials transportation standards. Content, formulas, tables, and original illustrations are bundled for offline study.

4. Memorization and Review

Users can hide highlighted keywords and tap to reveal them, answer concept-based recall questions, mark concepts completed, and bookmark materials for later review. Associated progress and preferences may be retained locally on the device.

5. Formulas, Tables, Images and Search

The App provides separate, enlargeable cards for formulas, tables, and original illustrations, along with text explanations of table values. Search operates on the App's bundled study content and highlights matching text. The described search and reading functions do not require sending queries to a Developer-operated server.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App shows overall and subject-level completion progress, supports continuation from the last-read concept, and allows users to retry incorrectly answered questions. These records are maintained through the App's local storage.

7. Device Permissions

Internet access may be used to load Home-screen advertisements. The described core study features do not require camera, microphone, contact-list, or photo-library access. Actual permissions and SDK access must be checked against the final released Android and iOS builds.

8. Local Data Storage

Study records and preferences are stored locally on the user's device. The supplied project description does not identify the precise local-storage library, so this policy does not claim a specific implementation. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it generally removes local records, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior.
