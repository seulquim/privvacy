Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Fire Protection Engineer (Electrical) Exam Study Notes (소방설비기사(전기) 필기 합격노트) (the "App").

The Android application ID is com.seul.fireelectricapp, and the current App version is 3.1.0.

The App is a Korean-language offline Flutter learning application designed to help users prepare for the Fire Protection Engineer (Electrical) examination.

Important: Core learning materials and study records are stored locally on the user's device. No account registration is required, and the Developer does not operate a server that receives individual learning records. The App uses Google AdMob for advertising. The advertising SDK may process certain device, network, diagnostic, and advertising-related information as described below.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, telephone number, or other personal contact details to use its core learning features.

The App processes the following learning information locally on the user's device:

  • Completed and previously viewed learning pages
  • Last visited learning page
  • Bookmarks
  • Incorrect answers and review records
  • Memorized flashcard status
  • Learning repetition counts
  • Practice results and accuracy statistics
  • Selected examination date
  • Theme and font-size preferences

This information is used to provide learning progress, personalized study continuity, review features, and user preferences.

The Developer does not receive these learning records through a developer-operated server.

2. Study Content

The App provides learning materials for the following four subjects:

  • Introduction to Fire Protection (소방원론)
  • General Electrical Engineering (소방전기일반)
  • Fire Protection Laws and Regulations (소방관련법규)
  • Fire Electrical Systems: Structure and Principles (소방전기시설의 구조 및 원리)

The bundled learning content includes:

  • 74 learning concepts
  • 437 concept practice questions
  • 466 memorization flashcards
  • 222 formula cards
  • Learning illustrations, diagrams, and explanatory tables

These materials are included in the App and can be accessed without an internet connection.

3. Practice Questions and Exam Results

The App provides subject-based practice questions, mock examinations, answer explanations, and incorrect-answer review features.

Mock examinations contain 20 questions per subject. The App may locally retain question results, incorrect-answer information, and accuracy statistics to support continued learning.

These records are stored on the user's device and are not uploaded to a Developer-operated server.

4. Memorization and Formula Cards

The App provides memorization flashcards, fill-in-the-blank learning, and formula review.

Memorization status and relevant study progress are stored locally to help users continue their learning across sessions.

5. Learning Progress and Preferences

The App provides study progress tracking, subject-level learning statistics, bookmarks, examination countdowns, and display preferences.

These settings and records are stored locally using the SharedPreferences storage mechanism.

The App does not require cloud synchronization or a user account for these features.

6. Offline Functionality

The App is designed to provide its primary learning functions without an internet connection.

Concept reading, flashcards, formula review, practice questions, and previously saved learning records can be accessed offline.

An internet connection may be required to load advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

7. Device Permissions

The core learning functions do not require access to the device camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.

The App may use internet connectivity and advertising-related permissions to display ads.

The exact permissions are determined by the final Android or iOS application build and its integrated dependencies.

8. Local Data Storage

Learning records and preferences are stored locally on the user's device using SharedPreferences.

The locally stored information may include learning progress, bookmarks, incorrect answers, memorization status, examination dates, and interface preferences.

These records are not transmitted to a Developer-operated cloud service.

Local information may be removed when users clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, subject to device backup and restoration settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process certain information for advertising delivery, performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

Depending on the device, SDK version, configuration, and applicable privacy settings, such information may include:

  • IP address
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • Advertising interactions
  • Application interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

For more information about Google's data practices, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Help

10. Advertising

The App displays a banner advertisement on the Home screen.

Advertisements are provided through Google AdMob on supported Android and iOS devices.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, network information, advertising interactions, and other technical data in accordance with their applicable privacy policies.

The availability of personalized advertisements and consent options depends on the App's advertising configuration, user region, and applicable requirements.

Advertisements are separate from the App's locally stored learning records. The App is not designed to transmit individual study progress or incorrect-answer records to Google AdMob.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied App configuration does not identify a separate developer-integrated analytics or crash-reporting service.

However, Google AdMob may process diagnostic, performance, and interaction data as part of its advertising services.

If additional analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

12. Data Sharing

The Developer does not operate a server that collects or shares individual learning records.

Locally stored study information, such as progress, bookmarks, and incorrect answers, is not intentionally sent to external services.

Advertising-related data may be transmitted to Google through the AdMob SDK as described in Sections 9 and 10.

The Developer does not sell users' locally stored learning records.

13. Data Security

The App relies on device and operating-system security mechanisms to protect locally stored learning information.

Users are encouraged to keep their devices and operating systems updated.

No electronic storage or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Learning records and preferences remain on the user's device until they are deleted or reset.

Users can reset their learning progress through the App's Settings screen.

Users may also remove local App data through device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Operating-system backup and restoration features may affect whether previously stored information can be restored.

Data processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is intended primarily for individuals preparing for the Fire Protection Engineer (Electrical) examination.

The App is not specifically designed for or directed to children under 13.

Advertising and related privacy controls must be configured according to the App's actual target audience and applicable requirements.

16. User Choices

Users can access the main learning features without creating an account.

Users may:

  • Change the display theme and font size
  • Manage bookmarks and memorization status
  • Reset learning progress through Settings
  • Clear locally stored App data
  • Uninstall the App

Where required and implemented, advertising privacy choices may be provided through Google-supported consent management mechanisms.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App functionality, third-party services, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
