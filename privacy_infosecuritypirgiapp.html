Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Information Security Engineer Exam (정보보안 기사필기) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.infosecuritypirgiapp.

The App is an offline-first Korean-language Flutter study tool with 134 concepts, 656 independently written practice questions, 907 flashcards, 67 comparison tables, 15 formulas, and 54 diagrams.

Important: Study records are stored locally using shared_preferences. The App uses Google AdMob banner advertising, which may process device and advertising information separately from offline study functions.

1. Information Processed by the App

No account registration or external API is required for the core learning features. The App processes study choices and progress locally on the user’s device.

  • Study unit progress, review counts, and bookmarks
  • Answers, scores, and incorrect-answer history
  • Flashcard familiarity and study preferences
  • Exam date, theme, and text-size preferences

2. Study Content and Practice Questions

The App provides independently written educational content across five exam subjects: System Security, Network Security, Application Security, General Information Security, and Information Security Management and Law. It includes 134 concepts and 656 independently created practice questions. These are not official examination questions.

Question responses are processed within the App for scoring and review.

3. Concepts, Tables, Formulas and Diagrams

The App includes 67 comparison tables, 15 formulas, and 54 in-app diagrams for educational purposes. Users may view and enlarge learning materials. No original textbook PDFs or page images are included.

4. Flashcards and Memorization

The App provides 907 flashcards. Memorization status, selected study material, and repetition records may be stored locally to support repeated learning.

5. Formula and Table Viewing

The App offers a dedicated formula-and-table section, including enlarged viewing. Display preferences and bookmarks may be stored locally.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App tracks completed concepts, incorrect answers, bookmarks, and review progress locally to help users revisit difficult topics.

7. Device Permissions

Internet access may be used to display Google AdMob advertisements. Core educational materials are designed for offline use.

The described study functions do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access. Actual permissions should be checked against the final release build and included SDKs.

8. Local Data Storage

The App uses shared_preferences to store study progress, bookmarks, incorrect answers, memorization status, review counts, exam date, light/dark mode, and text-size settings on the device.

Resetting learning records does not necessarily remove the saved exam date. Device backups may retain local information depending on operating-system settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob for banner advertisements. The App’s described core study features do not use a developer-operated account or cloud API.

Third-party services may process technical information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

The App may display a shared Google AdMob banner on its five main tabs. Advertising requires network connectivity and is separate from locally stored learning content.

Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers where permitted, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network data, device/app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics according to their policies.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project description does not establish that a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is enabled. Google AdMob and platform services may process technical or diagnostic information under their own policies. This policy will be updated if additional SDKs are introduced.

12. Data Sharing

The Developer does not operate a backend to upload the described study records. Advertising-related technical data may be transmitted to Google and its partners through the AdMob SDK.

Information may be disclosed where legally required, to the extent it is available to the Developer.

13. Data Security

Core learning records are stored on the user’s device. Users should protect their devices and maintain appropriate security settings.

No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. Advertising SDK communications are governed by the relevant providers’ practices.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records may remain until deleted using supported App controls, cleared through device settings, or removed by uninstalling the App, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

Third-party advertising information is retained and deleted under the respective providers’ policies.

15. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for individuals preparing for a professional information security examination and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

The App does not request personal information through an account registration system. Advertising age settings must match the actual audience and applicable rules.

16. User Choices

Users can manage study settings, bookmarks and supported learning records within the App, clear local App data using device settings, or uninstall the App.

Users may manage advertising identifiers and ad-personalization settings where supported by their device and jurisdiction.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy when App functionality, SDKs, laws, or platform requirements change. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
