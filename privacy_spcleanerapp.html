Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in 스피커 클리너 ("the App").

스피커 클리너 is an offline-first mobile application that generates specific sound patterns to vibrate a smartphone speaker and assist with removing moisture and light debris. The App also provides manual tone controls, speaker tests, cleaning history, local reminders, user presets, and local backup and restore features.

Offline-first design: The App does not require an account, sign-in, or a SeulStudio server for its core cleaning, manual playback, or sound-test functionality. App settings, cleaning history, user presets, scores, and notes are designed to remain on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses to share or copy backup data.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App may process and store the following information locally on the user's device:

  • App settings and theme preferences
  • Safe-output and playback settings
  • Cleaning history and usage records
  • Before-and-after perceived speaker condition scores
  • User-entered notes
  • Custom manual-mode presets
  • Last-used cleaning program
  • Reminder settings and reminder time
  • Locally generated statistics and score trends

The App does not require the user to provide a name, email address, phone number, postal address, or account credentials to use its core features.

2. Local Storage

App settings, records, presets, scores, notes, and reminder preferences are stored locally using SharedPreferences or equivalent platform-local storage.

This information is not uploaded to a SeulStudio server as part of the App's core functionality.

3. Audio Generation and Playback

The App uses flutter_soloud to generate audio waveforms in real time, including sine, triangle, and filtered square waves.

The App does not need to upload user audio files or stream cleaning sounds from a remote server. Cleaning, manual playback, and sound-test functions are designed to work offline.

The App may change frequency, waveform, output level, and duration according to the selected cleaning program or manual settings.

4. Audio Route Detection

To reduce the risk of playing cleaning tones through headphones, Bluetooth devices, or external audio devices, the App may inspect the current audio output route.

This information is used only to display a safety warning before playback and is not stored on a SeulStudio server.

5. Local Notifications

If enabled by the user, the App may schedule daily maintenance reminders using flutter_local_notifications.

Depending on the operating system version, notification permission may be required. On Android, the App may also use system capabilities required to restore scheduled reminders after a device restart.

Users can disable reminders in the App settings or through the operating system's notification settings.

6. Backup, Copy, Share, and Restore

The App may allow users to create text-based backups of local App data and restore those backups later.

The share feature uses share_plus and is activated only when the user explicitly chooses to share or copy backup data.

After data is shared with another app, messaging service, email service, cloud-storage provider, or other destination selected by the user, that third party's privacy practices apply.

7. Advertising

The App may display a Google AdMob banner advertisement on the Home tab.

The App does not use interstitial or rewarded advertisements, and banner advertising is not displayed on the cleaning, manual playback, or sound-test screens.

When AdMob is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process limited technical information such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • App version
  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted
  • Approximate location derived by the advertising service, where permitted
  • Ad interaction information
  • Advertising-related diagnostic or usage information

Advertising communications are separate from the App's local cleaning history, user presets, scores, notes, and backup data.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/

8. Network Use

Core cleaning, manual playback, and sound-test functions are designed to work without network access.

Network communication may occur when:

  • The Home tab loads a Google AdMob banner advertisement
  • The user shares backup data through a network-enabled third-party app

The App does not send cleaning history, user presets, scores, or notes to a SeulStudio server as part of normal App operation.

9. Permissions and System Access

Depending on the operating system and enabled features, the App may use or request:

  • Notification permission for local reminders
  • Boot-completed access on Android to restore scheduled reminders
  • Audio route information to warn about headphones or Bluetooth output
  • Screen wake or keep-screen-on behavior during certain playback flows

Permissions and system capabilities are used only for the corresponding App functionality.

10. Diagnostic and Sample Data

Debug builds may automatically populate sample records when the history is empty, and development settings may provide a manual option to add sample data.

Such sample records are local development and testing data and are not intended to represent or upload real user information.

11. Third-Party Services and Libraries

The App may use the following third-party services or libraries:

  • Google AdMob
  • flutter_soloud
  • flutter_local_notifications
  • share_plus
  • audio_session

Where a third-party service communicates over a network, its own terms and privacy policies may apply.

12. Data Sharing

SeulStudio does not sell users' cleaning history, presets, scores, notes, or backup data.

Local data is shared with another service only when the user intentionally uses a sharing or copy function, except for limited technical data independently processed by advertising services as described above.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by the App may remain on the device until the user deletes individual records where supported, clears App storage, restores or resets the device, or uninstalls the App.

Copies previously shared or saved outside the App must be deleted separately from the destination where they were stored.

Information processed by Google AdMob is retained and deleted in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

14. Data Security

The App is designed to reduce unnecessary transmission of user data by keeping core settings and history on the device.

No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their device and any backup data they choose to copy or share.

15. Safety and Functional Limitations

Important: 스피커 클리너 is a maintenance-assistance tool, not a hardware repair service. Results may vary depending on device design, speaker condition, moisture, contamination, volume level, and other physical factors.
  • Do not intentionally use the cleaning tones through headphones or external speakers.
  • Do not begin at maximum volume.
  • Stop immediately if severe vibration, distortion, crackling, or metallic noise occurs.
  • If the device is significantly wet or the charging port contains moisture, power off and allow the device to dry appropriately before use.
  • The App cannot repair corrosion, physical hardware damage, or a damaged speaker diaphragm.

16. Children's Privacy

The App is not primarily designed to collect personal information from children and does not require a SeulStudio account or centralized user profile.

If the App's target audience or functionality changes in a way that requires additional child-privacy measures, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

17. User Choices

Users can manage their data and privacy by:

  • Enabling or disabling local reminders
  • Managing notification permission in the operating system
  • Deleting supported local records
  • Clearing the App's local data
  • Deleting backup copies saved outside the App
  • Managing advertising preferences where supported
  • Uninstalling the App

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in App functionality, applicable law, Google Play or Apple App Store policies, or third-party services used by the App.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page will be revised.
