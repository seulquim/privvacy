Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Forklift Operator Exam (지게차운전기능사) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.forkpassapp.

The App is an offline Flutter learning tool for the Korean forklift operator examination. It includes 9 learning units, 66 topics, 635 review questions, a 120-question original practice bank plus 9 illustrated questions, CBT mock examinations, flashcards, tables, diagrams, and incorrect-answer review.

Important: Study progress and preferences are stored locally on the device using shared_preferences. Core learning features work offline. A Google AdMob banner may appear on the Home screen and may process advertising-related information independently of locally stored learning records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require account registration. Its core learning features do not require users to provide their name, email address, phone number, or contacts to the Developer.

The following learning data and settings are processed and stored on the user's device:

  • Study progress, completed topics, and memorization status (memorized / unsure / not memorized)
  • Bookmarks, flashcard review and learning preferences
  • Practice answers, CBT results, scores, and incorrect-answer history
  • Font size and other local settings

2. Quiz and Examination Practice

The App includes an original 120-question practice bank and 9 illustrated questions, as well as topic-based practice and a 60-question CBT-style mock examination with OMR-style navigation.

Answers are evaluated within the App, including a passing threshold of 60 out of 100 points and unit-level score summaries. Responses and results are retained locally to support review.

3. Learning Materials and Command Dictionary

The App provides 9 learning units covering 66 topics, including forklift safety, inspections, cargo handling, road driving, emergency response, equipment systems, and supplementary construction machinery topics.

Study materials include 101 tables, 4 formulas, 16 flowcharts, original diagrams, and 635 topic review questions. These materials are bundled with the App for offline use.

4. Flashcards and Memorization

The App offers flashcards based on topic review questions, hidden-keyword exercises, number fill-in practice, and memorization status tracking.

Memorization states and study preferences are stored locally to support repeat learning.

5. Calculation Tools

The App provides enlarged views of formulas and tables and study exercises involving numbers, symbols, and illustrations.

Interactions with these offline learning tools are handled on the device.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App provides an incorrect-answer notebook, question retry features, bookmarks, topic progress, and memorization-based review.

These records are stored locally on the device and can be reset using available App settings.

7. Device Permissions

The core learning functions operate without an internet connection. Network access may be used only to request and display the Google AdMob banner on the Home screen when advertising is enabled.

The documented learning features do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access. Permissions in the final distributed build should be verified separately, including permissions added by third-party SDKs.

8. Local Data Storage

The App uses shared_preferences to store memorization status, progress, bookmarks, incorrect answers, scores, and font-size settings on the device. The Developer does not operate a server to upload these learning records.

Users can reset supported study records through App settings, clear App storage through operating-system settings, or uninstall the App. Operating-system backups may retain local data according to the user's device configuration.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob for a single banner advertisement on the Home screen of supported Android and iOS devices, when enabled. Other study screens do not display advertisements according to the documented configuration.

Google and its partners may process technical and advertising information under their own policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

When advertising is enabled, the App displays one Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the Home screen. AdMob uses network access separately from the offline learning functions. The project currently specifies test advertising IDs, which must be replaced before live monetization.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device identifiers (including advertising IDs where available), network-inferred approximate location, advertisement interactions, app information, and diagnostics in accordance with their policies and applicable consent settings.

Disabling ads in the App's build configuration prevents advertising SDK initialization according to the documented implementation.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project description does not identify a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. This policy does not claim that such services are enabled.

Google AdMob and operating-system services may process diagnostic or technical information according to their own policies. This section should be revised if other SDKs are added.

12. Data Sharing

The Developer does not operate an account system or a backend service for uploading the App's locally stored study records.

When ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may receive or process technical and advertising-related data through their SDKs. Their processing is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Information may be disclosed where legally required, to the extent it is available to the Developer.

13. Data Security

Study records and preferences are stored on the device. Users should maintain appropriate device security and backup settings.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Advertising communications are subject to the security practices of the relevant third-party providers.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored study records may remain until the user resets them in the App, clears App data, or uninstalls the App, subject to device backup behavior.

Data processed by Google AdMob or platform providers is subject to those providers' retention and deletion policies.

15. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for individuals preparing for the Korean forklift operator written examination and is not specifically designed for children under 13.

The App does not request children's personal information through an account registration system. The Developer must configure any age-related advertising restrictions according to the actual target audience and applicable rules.

16. User Choices

Users can manage memorization states, bookmarks, and review records in the App, reset supported learning data, clear device App storage, or uninstall the App.

Where available, users may manage advertising identifiers and ad personalization through their device or Google settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, advertising SDKs, applicable laws, or platform requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
