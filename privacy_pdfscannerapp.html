Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in ChalckakPDF (찰칵PDF) (the "App"), a mobile document scanner and PDF editor. The project name is pdfscannerapp, the Android application ID and iOS bundle ID are com.seul.pdfscannerapp, and the version described is 1.0.0.

The App lets users scan paper documents, import images and PDFs, edit pages, recognize Korean and English text on-device, organize documents, and export or share PDFs.

Important: Documents and recognized text are designed to remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them. The App includes Google AdMob banner advertising on the Home tab; the advertising SDK may process data independently of your documents. This policy should be checked against the final release build, SDK configuration, permissions, and Google Play Data safety declarations before publication.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App processes photos taken for scanning, selected images and PDFs, edited pages, recognized text, document names, tags, favorites, creation dates, and document preferences to provide its functions. No account registration or developer-operated document upload server is described in the App's design.

2. Document Scanning and Camera

When you choose to scan a document, the App uses the device camera through its document-scanning functionality. It may detect document edges, correct perspective, and capture multiple pages. Captured pages are used to create and edit documents on your device. The App does not intentionally upload scanned document content to a developer-operated server.

3. Importing Images and PDFs

You may select images or existing PDF files through device file and media selection interfaces. The App may render imported PDF pages as images for editing and supports password-protected PDF import where the correct password is provided. Imported content is processed to perform the requested action. Files are not automatically shared with other users or uploaded to our servers.

4. Editing and PDF Generation

The App supports cropping, rotation, filters, brightness and contrast adjustment, page ordering, duplication, deletion, splitting, merging, and PDF generation. Draft documents can be saved for later editing. These operations are designed to run locally on your device.

5. On-Device Text Recognition (OCR)

The App uses Google ML Kit text recognition to identify Korean and English text on the device. Recognized text can be used for searching document contents and copying text. The described OCR workflow does not send document images or recognized text to a developer-operated server. Actual SDK behavior and any model downloads should be verified for the final release.

6. Document Library and Search

The App organizes documents using names, tags, favorites, dates, and draft status. Search may examine document names, tags, and locally recognized text. The document library includes list and calendar views. This information is used to help you find and manage documents on your device.

7. Device Permissions

Depending on the platform and selected feature, the App may request camera access and access to photos, media, or user-selected files. Internet and network-related permissions may be used for advertising. The App does not describe a need for microphone, contacts, or location access for core scanning functions. Actual requested permissions must be confirmed from the final release build.

8. Local Data Storage

Documents, pages, thumbnails, and recognized text are designed to be stored on the device. The App uses a local Drift/SQLite database for document metadata and may use local preferences for settings. Content remains subject to device storage management and operating-system backup or restore behavior. The Developer does not provide a cloud synchronization service for documents.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for banner advertising. It also uses platform and third-party libraries for document scanning, PDF processing, and on-device OCR. Third-party components may have their own data practices. For Google's privacy information, see Google Privacy Policy. For information about Google's advertising technologies, see How Google uses information in advertising.

10. Advertising

Google AdMob banner advertisements are displayed only on the Home tab, according to the described App design. AdMob may collect or process technical and advertising-related information such as IP address, device identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), app interactions, diagnostic data, and ad performance information, depending on SDK configuration, user consent, and applicable law. The Developer does not use document contents or recognized OCR text for ad targeting in the described implementation. Advertising may require an internet connection.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The described App does not include a separate developer-configured analytics or crash-reporting service. However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process diagnostics and performance information. Any additional analytics or crash-reporting SDKs added to a future release will require an update to this policy.

12. Data Sharing and User-Initiated Exports

The App does not automatically upload user documents to a Developer-operated server. When you choose Share, Save to device, Print, or export to another application, the selected content is provided to the destination you choose, and that destination's privacy practices may apply. Separately, Google AdMob may receive data needed to provide advertising, as described above.

13. Data Security and Password-Protected PDFs

The App provides an option to create a password-protected PDF copy using an AES-256-based PDF security implementation as described in the project documentation. This protection applies to the exported copy, not automatically to every local draft or imported document. Device-level security and safe handling of passwords remain important. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored documents and related metadata generally remain until you delete them, clear App data, or uninstall the App. The App provides document deletion and storage-cleanup features. Exported or shared copies are not necessarily removed when an original document is deleted. Operating-system backups and third-party destinations may retain copies. Google AdMob data retention is governed by Google's applicable practices and policies.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose document utility and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Advertising settings, age targeting, and any applicable parental or child privacy requirements must be configured appropriately before release.

16. User Choices

You may choose which documents to scan or import, edit or delete locally stored documents, and decide when to export or share files. You may manage applicable camera and media permissions through device settings. Where available, advertising identifier and privacy preferences can be managed through device settings or applicable consent controls. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data generally removes locally stored App data, subject to backups.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when App features, third-party services, legal obligations, or store requirements change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when changes are published.
