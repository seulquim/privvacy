Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in SQLD Study (SQLD 독립 학습) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.sqldapp.

The App is a Flutter-based, offline-first study aid for SQLD (SQL Developer) exam preparation. It contains 280 independently written study topics, 426 original multiple-choice practice questions, 79 learning tables, 24 mathematical expressions, 22 diagrams, and three original 50-question mock examinations. It is not an official examination app and does not provide actual past exam questions or officially certified questions.

Offline-first design: Core study materials, practice activities, progress, and exam drafts are available without an account or Developer-operated server. Study records and preferences are stored locally on the device. A Google AdMob banner may appear on the Home screen and may process advertising-related information separately from locally stored learning records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require an account or login, and its core features do not ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, or contacts to the Developer. It may process locally selected exam dates, study progress, memorization status (memorized, unsure, or not memorized), bookmarks, user-created flashcards, quiz answers, scores, incorrect-answer history, mock-exam drafts, and related preferences.

2. Practice Questions and Mock Examinations

The App offers 426 independently created four-choice practice questions and three original mock examinations, each consisting of 50 questions with a 90-minute time limit. OMR-style answer selection, scoring, subject-level minimum-score checks, incorrect-answer review, and exam-history features are designed to operate on the device. Answers, results, and unfinished exam drafts may be saved locally for continued study.

3. Learning Materials and Study Content

The App bundles 280 independently written topics, 79 explanatory tables, 24 mathematical expressions, and 22 diagrams. It provides SQL examples, reference information about exam structure, and study tools. The App does not include the original reference PDFs, links to textbook pages, or a source-material browsing menu. Learning content is accessible offline.

4. Memorization and Keyword-Hiding Features

Users can review highlighted study points, hide and reveal selected content, practice question-and-answer recall, mark memorization status, and maintain user-created flashcards. Related selections and records are stored locally.

5. Formulas, Tables, Diagrams, and SQL Practice

The App provides enlarged views of formulas and tables, explanations of table values, diagrams, and SQL learning exercises. These features use bundled learning content and local study interactions. Any SQL practice functionality should be understood as an educational feature rather than a connection to a Developer-hosted database service.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App may retain progress by topic, bookmarks, memorization levels, quiz results, incorrect-answer records, mock-exam history, and the user-selected examination date. These records support continued learning and personalized review and are stored locally on the device.

7. Device Permissions

Core study features do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or location access. Network permission may be used by Google AdMob and related consent services when advertising is enabled. The exact permissions and SDK declarations should be checked against the final release build before publication.

8. Local Data Storage and Backup

The App uses device-local storage for study progress and examination drafts, including storage keys sqld_study_v1 and sqld_exam_draft_v1. It also offers user-initiated JSON backup and restore. A JSON backup may contain learning records and preferences; users control where they save or share exported files. The Developer does not operate a cloud account or synchronization service for these records. Operating-system backup features may retain App data depending on device settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob for a banner advertisement on the Home screen. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process technical and advertising-related information in accordance with their policies and applicable consent settings.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

When advertising is enabled, the App may display a Google AdMob banner on the Home screen. Advertising services may process IP addresses, device identifiers (including advertising IDs where available), approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, app information, and diagnostics, subject to applicable consent choices and Google policies. Advertising uses network access independently of offline study features. The development project currently specifies Google test ad identifiers, which must be replaced and verified for production release.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project description does not identify a separate Developer-operated analytics service or a standalone crash-reporting SDK. Google AdMob and operating-system services may process technical or diagnostic information as described in their own policies. This section will be updated if other services are introduced.

12. Data Sharing

The Developer does not operate an account server that receives users' locally stored study records. If users voluntarily export or share a JSON backup, the recipient or destination is chosen by the user and may have its own privacy practices. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may receive or process technical and advertising-related data through their services. The Developer does not sell locally stored study records.

13. Data Security

Core study data is kept on the device rather than transmitted to a Developer-operated study server. Users should protect their devices and exported JSON backup files, which may contain study records. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Third-party advertising communications are subject to the providers' security practices.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored study records may remain until users reset supported records, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Exported JSON backup files remain wherever users have saved or shared them and must be deleted separately. Data processed by Google or other platform providers is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for learners preparing for the SQLD examination and is not specifically directed to children under 13. It does not provide an account-registration feature for collecting children's personal information. Advertising settings and any age-related protections must be configured according to the App's actual intended audience and applicable requirements.

16. User Choices

Users can manage study progress, memorization status, bookmarks, flashcards, exam dates, and available learning settings. They may export or restore a JSON backup, reset supported learning data where the App provides that option, clear App storage through operating-system settings, or uninstall the App. Advertising preferences and identifiers may be managed through applicable consent controls and device or Google settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in App functionality, third-party advertising services, applicable laws, or platform requirements. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
