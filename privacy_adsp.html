Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in ADsP Data Analysis (ADsP 데이터분석) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.adspapp.

The App is a Flutter-based offline educational study aid designed for individuals preparing for the Korean Advanced Data Analytics Semi-Professional (ADsP) certification examination.

The App includes 43 independently prepared study cards, 93 practice questions, 38 educational tables, 10 formulas and formula illustrations, 20 R programming and statistics reference items, memorization tools, CBT-style practice, and learning progress management.

Important: Learning records and preferences are stored locally on the device. Core study features work offline. A Google AdMob banner on the Home screen may process advertising-related and technical information independently of local study records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require an account or login. Its core learning features do not require users to submit their name, email address, telephone number, or contacts to the Developer.

The following information is processed locally:

  • Study progress and completed learning cards.
  • Subject-level learning progress.
  • Memorization status: memorized, unsure, or not memorized.
  • Practice question responses and results.
  • Selected examination date and D-day information.
  • Learning proficiency and study preferences.
  • Recently accessed learning content.

These records are used to provide learning continuity and personalized study management within the App.

2. CBT Practice and Mock Examinations

The App provides 93 independently created practice questions designed to support ADsP examination preparation.

Available practice features include:

  • A total of 93 practice questions.
  • Random practice consisting of 15 questions, with five questions from each subject.
  • Practice Examination A consisting of 50 questions.
  • OMR-style answer selection.
  • Answer explanations and related study concepts.
  • Practice results and learning progress management.

Question responses and practice results are processed on the device. Supported learning records are stored locally to assist users with continued study.

The practice questions are independently prepared educational materials and are not represented as official examination questions.

3. Learning Materials and Study Content

The App provides 43 independently prepared study cards covering the following three ADsP examination subjects:

  • Data Understanding (데이터 이해).
  • Data Analysis Planning (데이터 분석 기획).
  • Data Analysis (데이터 분석).

The educational content includes:

  • 43 core concept study cards.
  • 38 educational tables with value explanations.
  • 10 formulas and formula illustrations.
  • 93 independently created practice questions.
  • 20 R programming and statistics reference items.
  • Highlighted keywords and memorization questions.

Study content is bundled with the App and can be accessed offline without downloading additional learning materials from a Developer-operated server.

The App does not provide access to original textbook PDF files, original textbook pages, or original document-viewing links.

The App is an independently developed, unofficial educational resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the official ADsP examination authority.

4. Memorization and Keyword-Hiding Features

The App provides highlighted learning content, keyword-hiding exercises, and question-based memorization tools.

Users may:

  • Review highlighted key concepts.
  • Hide important keywords for active recall.
  • Reveal answers and related explanations.
  • Classify memorization status as memorized, unsure, or not memorized.
  • Review concepts requiring additional study.

Memorization choices and learning progress are stored locally on the device for repeated learning.

5. Formulas, Tables, and R Reference Materials

The App includes 38 educational tables, 10 formulas and formula illustrations, and 20 R programming and statistics reference items.

These materials support learning in statistics, data analysis, analytical methods, and R programming.

Users can review formulas, table values, related explanations, and R reference materials. Supported formulas and tables can be enlarged for easier reading.

These features access locally bundled educational content and do not require users to upload their study inputs to a Developer-operated server.

6. Learning Progress and Proficiency Management

The App provides learning progress management features to help users prepare for the examination.

These features include:

  • Overall learning progress.
  • Subject-level learning progress.
  • Learning proficiency management.
  • Recently accessed study content.
  • Resume learning functionality.
  • Examination date and D-day management.
  • Memorization status management.
  • Learning record reset functionality.

Supported learning records and preferences are stored locally on the device.

7. Device Permissions

The core educational features of the App operate offline.

Internet access may be used to load Google AdMob banner advertisements on the Home screen when advertising is enabled.

The described learning features do not require access to the device's camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or precise location.

Advertising SDKs may use network-related permissions and access advertising identifiers where permitted by the operating system and applicable user settings.

8. Local Data Storage

The App stores supported learning records and preferences locally on the user's device.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Study progress.
  • Completed learning cards.
  • Memorization status.
  • Practice results and learning records.
  • Selected examination date.
  • Learning preferences and App settings.

The Developer does not operate a user-account system or a backend service for uploading these local learning records.

Users can reset supported learning records through the App, clear App storage through operating-system settings, or uninstall the App.

Operating-system backup and restoration services may retain or restore locally stored information depending on the user's device settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements on the Home screen when advertising is enabled.

Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related and technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies, applicable laws, and user consent settings.

For additional information, please refer to the following third-party privacy resources:

  • App interaction information.
  • Technical diagnostics and performance information.

Such information may be used for advertising, advertising measurement, fraud prevention, security, and service operation.

The App does not intentionally provide locally stored study records, examination responses, or memorization records to advertising providers.

Where applicable, advertising personalization and consent options are governed by device settings, Google services, and applicable consent requirements.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored learning records and preferences may remain on the device until the user:
