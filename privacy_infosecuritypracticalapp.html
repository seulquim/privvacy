Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Information Security Engineer Practical Exam (정보보안 기사(실기)) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.infosecuritypracticalapp.

The App is an offline Flutter study aid for the Korean Information Security Engineer practical examination. It includes 11 subject areas, 65 study units, 1,189 question items (1,162 available for practice), 1,187 flashcards, 68 command and configuration blocks, 60 tables, and 62 illustrations.

Important: Study records and preferences are stored locally on the device using shared_preferences. Core study functions operate offline. A Google AdMob banner is displayed on the learning/Home tab when enabled, and advertising providers may independently process device and advertising information.

1. Information Processed by the App

No account registration or login is required. The core learning functions do not ask users to submit names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts to the Developer.

The App locally processes completed and read units, study progress, review cycles, bookmarks, memorization status, answers, mock-exam scores, incorrect-answer history, selected exam date, theme preference, and font-size settings.

2. Practice Questions and Mock Examinations

The App contains 1,189 question items, of which 1,162 are available for practice according to the supplied project description. It offers subject practice, incorrect-answer retries, and an 80-question mock examination with selectable time limits of 60, 90, 120, or 150 minutes.

Answer-sheet navigation, grading, results, and review operate on the device. Results and incorrect-answer records are stored locally for learning continuity. The displayed 60% reference threshold is study guidance, not a guarantee of passing the official examination.

3. Learning Materials and Study Content

The App contains 11 subjects and 65 study units, with concept explanations, 68 command and configuration blocks, 60 tables, and 62 illustrations. Materials are bundled for offline learning.

Users can browse units, mark them as read, navigate between units, search the full text, and use bookmarks. The App is an independent study aid and is not an official examination service.

4. Flashcards and Memorization

The App offers 1,187 flashcards. Users can mark a card as mastered or to review later, and optionally exclude mastered cards from subsequent review sessions.

Flashcard progress and memorization choices are saved on the device.

5. Commands, Configurations, Formulas and Tables

The App displays technical command/configuration examples, tables, formulas, and illustrations as educational content. Study interactions are processed on the device; the Developer does not operate a server to receive these learning inputs.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App records read status, subject progress, review cycles, bookmarks, incorrect answers, flashcard results, and examination-date settings locally.

Users may reset supported learning records through App settings; the selected examination date is retained when the documented learning-record reset is used.

7. Device Permissions

Core study functions work without an internet connection. Network access is used for Google AdMob advertising on the learning/Home tab when enabled.

The described learning features do not require access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library. Permissions introduced by third-party SDKs should be checked in the final release build.

8. Local Data Storage

The App uses shared_preferences to store study records and settings on the user’s device. The Developer does not operate an account service or backend to upload these learning records.

Users may clear supported records in App settings, clear App data through the operating system, or uninstall the App. Operating-system backups may retain local data depending on device settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob for an adaptive banner shown only on the learning/Home tab of supported Android and iOS builds when advertising is enabled. Web and desktop builds are documented as having advertising disabled.

Google and its partners may process technical and advertising information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

When enabled, a Google AdMob banner is shown at the bottom of the learning/Home tab. Advertising uses network access independently of offline study features.

Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses, device identifiers (including advertising IDs where available), network-inferred approximate location, advertising interactions, application information, and diagnostic information according to their policies and applicable consent choices.

The supplied project description indicates that test advertising identifiers are currently used and must be replaced with production identifiers before monetization.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project description does not identify a separately integrated analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google AdMob and operating-system services may process diagnostics and related technical information under their own policies. This section should be updated if additional services are added.

12. Data Sharing

The Developer does not run an account service or backend for collecting locally stored study records. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may receive or process advertising and technical information through their SDKs.

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, to the extent it is available to the Developer.

13. Data Security

Study records are stored locally on the device. Users should maintain suitable device security and backup settings. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. Third-party advertising communications are subject to the providers’ security practices.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored learning records may remain until reset through available App functions, cleared via device settings, or removed by uninstalling the App, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

Data processed by Google AdMob or other platform providers is retained and deleted according to those providers’ policies.

15. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for individuals preparing for the Korean Information Security Engineer practical examination and is not specifically designed for children under 13. It does not solicit children’s personal information through account registration.

Age-related advertising settings and compliance requirements must be configured according to the actual target audience and applicable rules.

16. User Choices

Users can manage bookmarks, flashcard status, learning records, examination date, theme, and font size in the App. They can reset supported records, clear device App data, or uninstall the App.

Where available, advertising identifiers and ad personalization can be managed through device or Google settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App functionality, third-party SDKs, laws, or platform requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
