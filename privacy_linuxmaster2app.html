Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Linux Master Level 2 (리눅스마스터2급) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.linuxmaster2app.

The App is a Flutter-based learning tool for Linux Master Level 2 written-examination preparation, featuring 963 practice questions, study summaries, a command dictionary, flashcards, calculators, and an incorrect-answer notebook.

Important: Study progress is stored on the device using shared_preferences. The App also integrates Google AdMob banner advertising, which may process device and advertising information independently of the App’s local study features.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require account registration. Its core study features do not require users to submit their name, email address, phone number, or contacts to the Developer.

The App processes user-selected study settings and responses to questions locally to provide learning functionality.

  • Selected study units and quiz settings
  • Question responses, scores, and incorrect-answer history
  • Flashcard progress and study preferences
  • Exam date and D-day settings

2. Quiz and Examination Practice

The App provides 963 practice questions for Linux Master Level 2 exam preparation. Questions may be selected by unit, frequency indicator, and quantity.

Question answers and results are processed to display explanations, scores, and review opportunities.

3. Learning Materials and Command Dictionary

The App includes unit summaries, a dictionary of 124 Linux commands, command flashcards, and 25 terminal practice exercises.

These learning materials are included for study purposes and do not require a user account.

4. Flashcards and Memorization

The App offers flashcards and memorization features that allow users to select study ranges and review learning material.

Preferences and progress may be retained on the device to support repeat learning.

5. Calculation Tools

The App includes chmod permission, subnet, RAID, and cron calculation tools.

Inputs are processed to calculate and display results within the App. The Developer does not operate a server to collect calculation inputs for these tools.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App offers an incorrect-answer notebook, review of previously missed questions, exam-date settings, and learning progress tracking.

The App uses locally stored records to provide these features.

7. Device Permissions

The App may use internet or network access to load advertisements and communicate with third-party advertising services.

The study functions described in the supplied project documentation do not require access to the device camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library. Actual permissions may vary with the final build and integrated SDKs.

8. Local Data Storage

The App uses shared_preferences to store study records and settings on the user’s device. These may include answer history, accuracy, incorrect answers, and the selected examination date.

Users can reset supported learning records in the App. They can also clear App storage or uninstall the App using device settings.

Device backups, if enabled by the operating system, may retain some locally stored App data according to device settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob for banner advertising on supported Android and iOS devices. Ads are not displayed in the web or desktop versions described in the project documentation.

Third-party SDKs may process technical information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

The App displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob on supported mobile platforms. The Developer’s project documentation indicates that test advertising IDs must be replaced with production IDs before monetization.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may collect or process data such as device identifiers (including advertising IDs where available), IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, app information, and diagnostics in accordance with their policies.

Advertising SDK network requests are separate from local quiz and study-record storage.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project documentation does not confirm the integration of a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. This policy does not claim that Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics is enabled.

Google AdMob and platform services may process diagnostics or related technical information as described in their own notices. If additional analytics services are introduced, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

12. Data Sharing

The Developer does not operate a user account service or a backend for uploading the study records described above.

Advertising partners such as Google may receive or process technical and advertising-related information through their SDKs. Their processing is governed by their respective policies.

The Developer may disclose information if required by applicable law and if such information is available to the Developer.

13. Data Security

Study progress and preferences are stored locally on the device through the App’s storage mechanisms. Users should protect their devices and maintain appropriate device security settings.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Third-party advertising communications are subject to the relevant providers’ security practices.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored study records may remain on the device until they are reset using available App functions, cleared through operating-system settings, or removed by uninstalling the App, subject to any applicable device backup behavior.

Data processed by Google AdMob or other platform services is retained and deleted under those providers’ policies.

15. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended as a study aid for individuals preparing for the Linux Master Level 2 examination and is not specifically designed for children under 13.

The Developer does not knowingly request children’s personal information through an App account system. Age-related advertising settings and obligations must be configured in accordance with the actual target audience and applicable platform rules.

16. User Choices

Users may review and reset learning records within the App, clear locally stored App data, or uninstall the App.

Where supported by their device and jurisdiction, users may manage advertising identifiers and advertising personalization preferences through device or Google settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s functionality, SDKs, applicable law, or platform requirements change.

The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
