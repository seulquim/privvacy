Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how resignstampapp ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Today I Quit (오늘 퇴사), package name com.seul.resignstampapp (the "App"). The App is a local-first tool for creating resignation letters, virtual stamps and handwritten signatures, and for privately recording resignation urges.

No account is required. Resignation documents and private mood records are kept separate within the App and are not combined in exported resignation-letter PDFs.

1. Information Handled by the App

The App may handle information that users choose to enter or create, including:

  • Resignation-letter content, drafts, dates, reasons, and related document details
  • Virtual stamp designs, stamp text, style settings, and exported stamp images
  • Handwritten signatures drawn by the user and saved signature images
  • Resignation-urge scores, selected reasons, private notes, and recent history
  • App settings, preferences, and document archive information

This user-created content is stored locally on the user's device using local app storage and files. We do not operate an account system or our own server for collecting or storing this content.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal or confidential information. The App does not require access to contacts, call logs, SMS, precise location, health data, or payment information for its core features.

2. Separation of Documents and Private Records

Resignation letters, virtual stamps, and signatures are document-related data. Resignation-urge scores, reasons, and notes are private records. These categories are handled separately in the App.

Private resignation-urge records are not automatically inserted into, attached to, or included in resignation-letter PDFs or document-sharing actions. Only content selected for the resignation letter, together with an optionally placed stamp or signature, is included in the document.

3. Information Processed by Third-Party Services

The App may use Google Play Services and Google AdMob to display and measure banner advertisements. Depending on device settings, regional requirements, user choices, and Google's implementation, these services may automatically process information such as:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, app version, and general device identifiers
  • Advertising ID or other advertising-related identifiers, where available and permitted
  • App interactions and advertising events, such as ad impressions, clicks, and session-related activity
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash, performance, and error data associated with the advertising service
  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address or similar network information

The Developer does not use this third-party advertising data to directly identify individual users. Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App may be used to:

  • Create, edit, preview, save, duplicate, search, and delete resignation letters
  • Generate resignation-letter PDFs and stamp or signature PNG files
  • Display D-day information and recent document information
  • Save virtual stamp designs and handwritten signatures for later use
  • Record and display private resignation-urge history and seven-day trends
  • Save App settings and restore locally stored drafts
  • Display and measure advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Maintain App stability, security, and compliance with applicable policies

5. Local Storage

Resignation letters, drafts, stamps, signatures, resignation-urge records, and preferences are stored in the App's local storage or local files on the user's device. This content is not uploaded to a server operated by the Developer.

Local data may be accessible to someone who gains access to the unlocked device or its storage. Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security, such as a screen lock and secure device access.

6. PDF, PNG, and Sharing Features

When a user chooses to export or share a resignation-letter PDF, stamp PNG, or signature-related document, the selected file is transferred only through the destination or sharing service chosen by the user. Such a transfer is initiated by the user.

After a file leaves the App, it may be stored or processed by the selected email, messaging, cloud-storage, printing, file-management, or other third-party service according to that service's privacy practices. The App cannot delete or control copies held outside the App.

Private resignation-urge scores, reasons, and notes are not included in resignation-letter PDFs or standard document-sharing output.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

  • Google AdMob Privacy and Data: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

8. Advertising and User Choices

The App may display banner advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may use device information, advertising identifiers, app activity, and other signals to provide, limit, measure, protect, and improve advertisements, subject to applicable law, device settings, and user consent choices.

Depending on the Android version and region, users may be able to reset or delete the Advertising ID, limit ad personalization, or manage privacy and advertising choices through Android or Google settings.

We do not sell personal or sensitive user information.

9. Data Sharing

We do not share user-created resignation letters, stamps, signatures, or private resignation-urge records with our own server or with advertisers. These items leave the App only when the user intentionally exports or shares them through a service selected by the user.

Google AdMob and Google Play Services may process limited device, advertising, interaction, diagnostic, and approximate-location data as described above. Their processing is governed by their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit data handling. User-created content is kept in local App storage, and the Developer does not maintain a centralized account database for this content.

Information transmitted by third-party services or through user-initiated sharing is handled according to the security practices of the relevant provider. No method of local storage or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local App data remains on the device until the user deletes individual items, uses the Delete All Local Data function in the App, clears the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstalls the App.

Deleting App data does not delete PDFs, PNG files, printed copies, backups, or files that were previously exported, saved outside the App, or shared with another service or person. Users must delete those copies from their storage locations or request deletion from the applicable recipient or service.

Data processed by Google AdMob or Google Play Services is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable retention practices and privacy policies.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or Developer-operated server.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided information requiring action, please contact us. If the App's target audience changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and take the steps required by applicable child privacy laws and Google Play policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
