Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Industrial Safety Engineer Exam (산업안전기사 필기) (the "App"). The project name is indusafepirgiapp, and the Android application ID is com.seul.indusafepirgiapp. The App is developed using Flutter 3 and Dart 3.

The App is a Korean-language study tool for the Industrial Safety Engineer examination. It offers subject-based concepts, original practice quizzes, past-exam topic references, mock tests, safety diagrams, formulas, tables, legal reference material, memorization, and wrong-answer review. Core study materials are designed for offline use.

Release verification notice: The supplied project information confirms local study-state storage using SharedPreferences, but does not establish whether advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud backup, or other third-party SDKs are included in the final release. Verify the release build, its permissions and dependencies, and update this policy before publication.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App manages study-related information such as completed topics, exam date, last opened topic, last study date, memorization status, personal notes, consecutive study days, and wrong-question identifiers. The supplied description does not identify mandatory account registration or a developer-operated account server.

2. Practice Questions and Answers

The App includes 777 independently prepared study quizzes, including 220 questions identified as repeated or frequently tested concepts, and exam-related practice sessions. User selections, completion status, and wrong-question identifiers may be processed on the device to provide scoring and review. Whether individual answers and historical mock-test scores are retained must be checked in the release build.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App provides 626 concept cards, 177 formulas, 131 summary tables, 119 original diagrams, 48 workplace scenarios, 138 legal-reference entries, and 43 safety signs. It organizes content by examination subjects and supports study of updated subject mappings. These learning materials are bundled for offline access. References to past examination coverage do not mean that all indexed source questions are reproduced in the App.

4. Memorization and Review

The App supports memorization status, repeated review, and wrong-answer study. Memorization state and wrong-question identifiers are among the categories recorded in the local study state. These records help users resume and organize learning.

5. Formulas, Diagrams, and Legal References

The App displays formulas, tables, safety illustrations, and legal reference materials for educational purposes. Any validation of source PDFs or content integrity performed during development is a development process and does not, by itself, involve transmission of a user's personal study data.

6. Learning Records

Based on the project description, the App uses local keys corresponding to completed, examDate, lastOpenedTopicId, lastStudyDate, memory, notes, streakDays, and wrongQuizIds. These support progress tracking, study continuity, memorization, notes, and wrong-answer review. The exact data format and any additional stored fields should be confirmed against the published build.

7. Device Permissions

Core offline study functions do not inherently require camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location access. Actual permissions, including internet access and any advertising-related permissions, depend on the final application manifest and included SDKs. The Developer will describe any additional permissions used in the published version.

This is local application storage, not proof that data never leaves the device: operating-system backup or third-party SDK behavior may differ. Local data may be removed when application data is cleared or the App is uninstalled, subject to device backup and restoration settings.
