Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Computer Specialist Level 1 Exam (컴퓨터활용능력 1급 필기) (the "App"), Android package ID and iOS bundle ID com.seul.comhwalpirgiapp

The App is a Korean-language offline Flutter study application for the Computer Specialist Level 1 examination. It covers 80 study topics across Computer Fundamentals, Spreadsheet Fundamentals, and Database Fundamentals. It provides study materials, practice and mock examinations, memorization cards, incorrect-answer review, and learning management features.

Important: Core study materials are designed for offline use. The App handles learning progress, practice results, memorization status, and study preferences. Users may export and import learning-record files. The provided project description does not establish whether advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or remote data transmission are enabled. These points must be verified against the final release before publication.

1. Information Processed by the App

To provide its study and record-management functions, the App may process:

  • Completed study topics, progress percentages, and last-read topic
  • Practice answers, examination scores, incorrect-answer history, and bookmarked questions
  • Automatically generated and user-created memorization cards, including review ratings and scheduled review dates
  • Examination date, D-day, study streak, study duration, and estimated scores
  • Theme and question-option-shuffling preferences
  • Learning records selected by users for export or import

The supplied project description does not specify an account registration or cloud synchronization service. Any such service added to the released App must be reflected in this policy.

2. Quiz and CBT Examination Practice

The App offers fixed mock examinations A and B with 60 questions each (20 per subject), randomized mock examinations, subject-based and topic-based practice, examination and practice modes, an OMR-style answer sheet, and score and subject-failure indicators. Practice responses and results are used to provide feedback, explanations, and related-topic navigation. The educational questions are not guaranteed to match official examination papers.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App covers 80 topics across Computer Fundamentals, Spreadsheet Fundamentals, and Database Fundamentals. Content includes concepts, tables, formulas, diagrams, keywords, functions, and shortcuts. Bundled study materials are designed to be accessible offline.

4. Memorization Cards and Spaced Review

Memorization cards may be generated from learning materials or created by users. Users can mark cards as known, uncertain, or unknown. The App schedules follow-up review based on those ratings and may retain card contents and review status for future study.

5. Search and Self-Assessment

The App provides search across study concepts, tables, and memorization questions, as well as self-assessment activities such as reviewing key sentences and checking key terms in a user's own explanation. The exact storage and handling of entered explanations, if any, should be confirmed against the released implementation.

6. Learning Progress and Incorrect Answers

The App tracks topic completion, subject progress, last-read content, study duration, streaks, and examination results. Incorrectly answered questions may be collected for later review and removed from the incorrect-answer list when answered correctly, as described in the App's functionality.

7. Device Permissions

The described core learning features do not inherently require access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location. Exporting and importing learning-record files may involve operating-system file selection or sharing interfaces. Actual permissions and SDK access must be checked in the final Android and iOS builds.
