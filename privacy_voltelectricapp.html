Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Electric Engineer Exam (전기기사 필기) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.voltelectricapp.

The App is a Flutter-based study aid for the Korean Electric Engineer (전기기사) and Industrial Electric Engineer (전기산업기사) examinations. It provides offline study cards, past-exam question practice, CBT-style examinations, memorization tools, formula references, and incorrect-answer review.

Offline-first design: Study progress, quiz results, memorization states, examination dates, and preferences are stored on the user's device. Core learning features do not require an account or Developer-operated cloud service. When Google AdMob advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process advertising-related data separately from these local study records.

1. Information Processed by the App

The App does not require an account or login for its core learning features and does not ask users to provide their name, email address, telephone number, or contacts to the Developer. It processes study progress, memorization status, question responses, scores, incorrect-answer history, selected examination date, display settings, and study preferences locally on the device.

2. Past Questions and CBT Practice

The App includes a question bank of 3,400 questions across 34 examination sessions from 2015 to 2026. Based on the current project description, 17 questions are held for review and excluded from normal scored practice, leaving 3,383 available for solving. Users can practice by examination session or subject, complete OMR-style tests, take timed 100-question mock examinations, and use ten fixed practice sets (A–J). Answers, scores, and related progress are processed locally.

3. Study Cards and Learning Materials

The App provides 96 subject-based study cards covering circuit theory, control engineering, electromagnetics, electrical machines, electric power engineering, and KEC electrical installation standards. The App also includes locally bundled diagrams, formulas, tables, electrical symbols, and supplementary learning materials. Selecting the Industrial Electric Engineer examination mode excludes control engineering content as described in the App.

4. Memorization and Keyword-Hiding Features

Users can review highlighted concepts, hide and reveal keywords, and categorize learning items by memorization status. These selections and associated progress are stored locally for future review.

5. Formulas, Diagrams, and Reference Tools

The App provides formula and table references, electrical-symbol references, KEC study summaries, a formula calculator, and unit-conversion tools. Inputs to these local study tools are not intentionally uploaded to a SeulStudio server.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

The App may retain incorrect-answer history, repeated-question history, examination-session results, memorization states, subject progress, and a user-selected examination date. This information is used to support review, D-day tracking, and continued learning on the device.

7. Device Permissions

Core learning functions are designed to operate offline. Internet access may be used for Google AdMob advertising and any associated consent flow when advertising is enabled. The described study functions do not require camera, microphone, or contacts access. Actual permissions, including those contributed by third-party SDKs, must be checked against the production Android and iOS builds.

8. Local Data Storage

Learning records and preferences are saved locally on the device using the App's local persistence mechanism. The Developer does not operate a user-account backend for uploading these study records. Supported records may be reset within the App. Users can also clear App data through operating-system settings or uninstall the App. Depending on device settings, operating-system backups may preserve some local data.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob through the google_mobile_ads package to support banner advertising on configured screens. Google and its advertising partners may process technical, device, and advertising information under their own policies and applicable consent choices.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Advertising

When advertising is enabled, banner ads may appear on supported App screens. Ad services may process IP addresses, device identifiers (including advertising IDs where available), approximate location inferred from network information, advertising interactions, app information, and diagnostics, subject to Google policies, platform settings, and applicable consent requirements. Advertising requires network access, but core study functions do not. The supplied project description indicates that test AdMob identifiers are currently configured and must be replaced and validated before production release.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The supplied project description does not identify a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. Google AdMob and operating-system providers may process technical and diagnostic data as described in their policies. This policy should be updated if other analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services are added.

12. Data Sharing

SeulStudio does not operate a backend for receiving users' locally stored study answers, scores, or memorization records. When ads are enabled, Google and its partners may receive or process advertising-related and technical data through their services. The Developer does not sell locally stored learning records.

13. Data Security

Core study records are stored on the device rather than intentionally transmitted to a SeulStudio server. Users should maintain appropriate device security and backup settings. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Advertising communications are subject to the practices of the relevant third-party services.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored learning data may remain until the user resets supported records, clears App storage, or uninstalls the App, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Information processed by Google AdMob or platform providers is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for people preparing for professional electrical qualification examinations and is not specifically directed to children under 13. It does not require account registration or request children's personal information for core study features. Advertising settings and any age-related protections must reflect the App's actual target audience and applicable requirements.

16. User Choices

Users can adjust study preferences, memorization states, examination date, display settings, and supported learning records within the App. They may clear App data or uninstall the App. Where available, users may manage advertising identifiers, personalization, and consent through device, Google, or in-App controls.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in App functionality, advertising services, applicable law, or platform requirements. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
