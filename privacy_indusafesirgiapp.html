Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Industrial Safety Engineer Practical Study Notes (산업안전기사 실기) (the "App"). The project name is indusafesirgiapp. The Android application ID and iOS bundle identifier are com.seul.indusafesirgiapp.

The App is a Korean-language, offline-first study application for the Industrial Safety Engineer practical examination, covering written-response (필답형) and performance-based (작업형) preparation. It includes 1,186 concept cards, 1,200 independent practice questions, frequently tested topics, mock examinations, formulas, reference tables, diagrams, safety signs, and relevant legal-reference study materials.

Release verification notice: The described core study functions operate offline, and learning records are designed to be saved on the device using SharedPreferences. Advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, operating-system backup behavior, and actual network permissions have not been verified against the final release build. These must be reviewed, and this policy amended where necessary, before publication.

1. Information Processed by the App

The described App does not require account registration for its core study features. It may process learning progress, completed concept identifiers, memorization ratings (known, uncertain, unknown), notes, incorrect-answer identifiers, mock-exam results, last-opened learning position, exam target date, and consecutive study days as part of its local study functions. The exact persisted fields should be checked against the final implementation.

2. Practice Questions and Answers

The App provides approximately 1,200 independently prepared practice questions, including short-answer, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, situation-based, and calculation exercises. It may process answers and grading outcomes locally to provide feedback, review, and incorrect-answer practice. Mock examination scores are study aids and do not represent official examination results.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App includes 1,186 concept cards covering industrial safety management, machinery and equipment safety, electrical and chemical safety, construction safety, calculations, and performance-based safety tasks. Reference materials include 14 formulas, 134 tables, 20 diagrams, 94 legal-reference entries, and 43 safety signs. These are bundled learning materials rather than personal information collected from users.

4. Memorization and Review

The App allows users to mark concepts as known, uncertain, or unknown, track completion, and review frequently tested topics. These choices may be stored locally to support personalized review and progress tracking.

5. Calculations, Legal References and Safety Materials

The App displays industrial safety formulas, worked calculations, diagrams, safety signs, and summaries of legal and technical standards. These materials are intended for education. The App's description does not indicate that calculations or study inputs are submitted to a remote processing service. Laws and technical standards may change; users should verify current requirements with official sources.

6. Learning Records and Mock Examinations

The App includes progress tracking, incorrect-answer review, timed mock examinations, exam-day countdown, and study streaks. Relevant records may be saved on the device. The precise storage and reset behavior must be confirmed in the final release build.

7. Device Permissions
