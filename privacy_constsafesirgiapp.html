Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Construction Safety Engineer Practical Exam (건설안전기사 실기노트) ("the App"), package ID com.seul.constsafesirgiapp, version 11.4.0.

The App is an offline Flutter learning application for the Korean Construction Safety Engineer practical examination. It includes 18 chapters, 257 core study items, 750 detailed concept cards, 267 practice questions, legal-reference tables, safety signs, formulas, study tables, and original learning illustrations.

Important: The App is designed for offline study. Study progress, memorization classifications, practice answers, notes, and exam-date settings may be stored locally on the device. The supplied project description does not confirm whether advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the final release. Please verify those integrations before publication.

1. Information Processed by the App

The described learning features do not require an account or submission of a name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

  • Study progress and completed learning items
  • Memorization status (known, uncertain, unknown) and review progress
  • Practice responses, results, and incorrect-answer records
  • User-created study notes
  • Selected examination date, D-day, and display preferences

2. Quiz and Practical Examination Preparation

The App includes 267 concept-check questions across 18 chapters, with chapter selection, randomized answer order, scoring, explanations, and incorrect-answer review. These are learning exercises and are not represented as official examination papers.

3. Learning Materials and Subject Content

The App contains 257 core study items and 750 detailed concept cards organized into 18 chapters. Cards may be tagged by concept, past-exam topic, or practical-work type. The App also includes safety and legal-reference materials for study.

4. Memorization and Study Notes

The App supports highlighted text, hidden-keyword practice, question-based memorization, notes, and known/uncertain/unknown classifications. Users can review items marked uncertain or unknown.

5. Formulas, Tables, Figures, and Search

Materials include 12 accident-indicator formulas, 24 study tables, 60 concept tables, 34 original illustrations, and 43 safety signs. The App provides chapter/type filters, search, and enlarged table viewing.

6. Incorrect Answers and Learning Progress

Practice answers and review status may be processed on the device to show scores, explanations, and items requiring further study.

7. Device Permissions

The described core learning features do not require camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access. Any network permissions or SDK-specific permissions must be confirmed against the final Android and iOS builds.
