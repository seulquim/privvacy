Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in 판옥선: 조선의 바다 (Panokseon: Seas of Joseon) ("the Game").

The Game is a landscape-oriented, 2.5D naval action game for Android, developed with Flutter and Dart. It features a 100-stage campaign, naval combat, expeditions, progression, and local save data.

Offline gameplay: Core gameplay works without an internet connection, account, or SeulStudio server. Game progress and preferences are saved locally on the device. The Game includes Google AdMob banner advertising, which may use an internet connection and process data as described below.

1. Information Processed by the Game

The Game processes gameplay information such as completed stages, earned stars, resources, ship and crew upgrades, unlocked commanders and equipment, achievements, expedition and conquest progress, battle records, cosmetic selections, daily challenge records, and preferences such as difficulty, sound levels, controls, accessibility, and frame-rate settings.

The Game does not require a name, email address, phone number, postal address, or SeulStudio account to play. Advertising services may separately process device or advertising-related data.

2. Local Storage

Game progress, settings, and related gameplay records are stored on the user's device using Android app storage and Flutter SharedPreferences (save key: panokseon_save_v3). The Developer does not operate a cloud save service for these records, and core save data is not routinely uploaded to a SeulStudio server.

3. Gameplay and Progression

The Game may record campaign completion, battle outcomes, scores, stage ratings, survival records, daily challenges, ship and crew progression, inventory, unlocked content, and expedition states locally to provide continuity between play sessions.

4. Save Backup and Restore Codes

The Game allows users to export locally saved progress into a text-based backup code beginning with PNKS1. and restore progress by entering such a code. Backup codes are handled by the user and are not automatically uploaded to a SeulStudio server. Users should keep backup codes private and share them only when they intend to share their game progress.

5. Advertising and Consent

The Game includes Google AdMob banner advertisements on campaign/expedition and battle screens. The advertising SDK may process information such as advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to applicable consent choices and Google policies.

Where required, the Game uses a consent flow before initializing or requesting advertisements. Consent and privacy options depend on the user's region, applicable law, and SDK configuration. Declining consent where offered does not prevent offline gameplay, although ad availability or personalization may differ.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/

6. Network Use

The campaign, battles, expeditions, local progression, settings, and backup/restore functions are designed to operate without network access. Internet access may be used by Google AdMob and its consent-management services. An unavailable network or failed ad request does not prevent core gameplay.

7. Permissions and Device Access

  • Internet/network state: Used by the advertising and consent services.
  • Advertising identifier: May be accessed by advertising SDKs when available and allowed by Android settings, SDK configuration, and applicable consent.
  • Audio and vibration: Used for game sound and haptic feedback according to device support and user settings.
  • Local app storage: Used to retain progress and preferences on the device.

The exact Android permissions are determined by the final app manifest, including permissions contributed by third-party SDKs.

8. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

SeulStudio does not sell locally stored campaign progress, battle records, or backup codes. The Game integrates Google AdMob, which may collect or share advertising-related data with Google and its partners in accordance with their policies and applicable consent. The Developer does not independently operate an analytics account system or remote game-progress database for this Game.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data generally remains until the user resets or deletes supported records, clears the Game's app storage, or uninstalls the Game, subject to any device-level backup and restore behavior. Exported backup codes remain wherever the user chooses to store them and must be deleted separately. Data processed by Google advertising services is retained under Google's applicable policies and controls.

10. Data Security

Keeping core game records locally reduces the need to transmit gameplay data. Advertising-related network communication is managed by third-party SDKs. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting any backup codes they export.

11. Children's Privacy

The Game does not require a user account or ask players to provide personal contact information to SeulStudio. Advertising practices and any age-related ad restrictions are governed by the Game's intended audience configuration, applicable law, and Google Play and AdMob policies. Parents or guardians should review device privacy and advertising settings where appropriate.

12. User Choices

  • Play the core game without an internet connection or SeulStudio account.
  • Adjust audio, vibration, controls, display, and accessibility preferences.
  • Export and restore game progress using a backup code.
  • Clear locally stored Game data through Android app settings or uninstall the Game.
  • Manage advertising consent or privacy choices when such controls are offered in the Game, and manage device-level advertising settings.

13. Historical and Gameplay Content

Content notice: The Game is a fictionalized entertainment experience inspired by historical naval battles. Some events, weapons, characters, mechanics, and post-war campaigns are fictional or adapted for gameplay. It is not an official historical reference.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in Game functionality, advertising SDK behavior, applicable law, or platform requirements. The "Last updated" date will be revised when the policy changes.
