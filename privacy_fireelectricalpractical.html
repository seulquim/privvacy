Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeulStudio ("we", "our", or "the Developer") handles information in Fire Protection Systems Engineer Practical (Electrical) Study Notes (소방설비기사 실기(전기)) (the "App"), a Korean-language Flutter study application for the Fire Protection Systems Engineer (Electrical Field) practical examination. Its Android application ID is com.seul.fireelectricalpractical. The documented App version is 2.110.0+38.

Pre-release notice: The App is currently marked RELEASE_BLOCKED pending content verification. This policy reflects the provided project description, not an independent audit of the final release build. Local storage implementation, permissions, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, and network behavior must be checked before publication. This notice should be updated once verified.

1. Information Processed by the App

The described App does not include an account-registration or sign-in feature, and no developer-operated cloud service is identified. To support learning, it manages study status (such as remembered, uncertain, or unknown), spaced-review progress, daily goals, exam dates, and calculation practice results. The precise fields retained and their storage implementation must be confirmed in the release build.

2. Learning Content and Practice

The App includes nine learning sections, 440 concept and question entries, 147 tables, 100 calculation exercises, 29 formulas, 21 independently created figures, and 17 legal or technical-standard entries. The content is bundled for offline study. Original reference PDF and HWP files, verbatim source question choices, and original captured images are not distributed in the App.

3. Memorization and Spaced Review

Users can mark learning items as remembered, uncertain, or unknown, and practice using spaced repetition, fill-in-the-blank activities, and blank-page recall. These features use learning status and review information on the device. Whether any such records are transmitted externally must be verified against the release build and its integrated SDKs.

4. Calculations and Assessment

The App offers calculation exercises and answer checking. Exercise inputs and results may be processed to provide feedback and learning progress. The supplied project description does not establish the exact persistence or retention of individual calculation responses.

5. Study Goals and Records

Users can configure an exam date and daily study goal, and track learning progress. The App supports a JSON-based learning-record backup and restore workflow through the clipboard. Information copied to the clipboard may be accessible to the operating system and other software according to device settings and platform behavior. Users should avoid sharing backup data with untrusted recipients.

6. Offline Operation

Core learning materials and study features are designed to function without an internet connection. Offline availability does not by itself establish that third-party SDKs, if present, never communicate over a network.

7. Device Permissions

The described learning features do not inherently require access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or location. The permissions actually requested by the App, including any network-related permissions, must be verified in the final Android manifest and release build.

8. Local Storage and Backups

The App maintains learning state and offers JSON backup and restore. The project description does not confirm the underlying persistence technology, backup encryption, or whether operating-system cloud backup is enabled. Exported or copied backups are controlled by the user and may remain outside the App after the App is uninstalled.
